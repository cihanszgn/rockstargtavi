Fortnite v43.10 Treasure Hunters update is out

The update adds a Locker grid density button, a High Quality Assets option for PC and new Sprites.

According to Epic Games' v43.10 Treasure Hunters update notes, a density button for the Locker grid was added, along with a High Quality Assets option for PC in the Epic launcher. New Sprites and the Buggy Captain's Day arrive on November 12, Mini Miku and Squiplash on November 15, and Captain's Day Miku and Teto also on November 15. Cursed Sprites are boosted on November 14.
