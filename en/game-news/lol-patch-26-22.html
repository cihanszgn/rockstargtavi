League of Legends 26.22: K/DA skins, a Mel update and balance changes

Patch 26.22 brings K/DA skins, a Mel gameplay update and ARAM Mayhem changes.

November 4, 2026Source: League of Legends (Riot Games)
LoL
Image: League of Legends (Riot Games)Update

According to Riot Games' 26.22 patch notes, K/DA skins and a gameplay update for Mel arrive, along with changes to ARAM Mayhem. In Summoner's Rift balance, Corki and Rumble are buffed while Locke and Yasuo are nerfed; Doran's Bow and Gluttonous Greaves are also adjusted.
