Dead by Daylight adds Terrifier's Art the Clown

Art the Clown arrives on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One today, bringing a bag of random weapons and emote-powered executions to the game.

November 24, 2026Source: Xbox Wire
DbD
Image: Xbox WireUpdate

Dead by Daylight has added Art the Clown from The Terrifier films as its latest Killer, and the character is out today on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One. According to Xbox Wire, Art never speaks in the films, so in the game he relies on big gestures and exaggerated expressions instead.

Art's power, Bag of Toys, pulls a random weapon from his trash bag: a flail, a flamethrower, a firearm, a beer bottle full of acid or his bike horn. Every successful hit with a toy makes the weapon deadlier, and Art can execute injured Survivors. Each weapon has its own Mini-Mori, and two extra finishers bring the total to seven executions, a new record for the series.

Emotes matter too. Art can emote at any time, but emoting at a Survivor after downing or hooking them increases the deadliness of all his weapons, according to Principal Killer Designer Janick Neveu. Perks such as Laughingstock, Kill Spree and Funny Business reward big, over-the-top plays, so players who want Art at his best will have to act like a clown.
