Mario Kart World 1.9.0 adds 8 new courses and 2 Knockout routes

Released on November 24 for Nintendo Switch 2, the update brings remixed courses, new Knockout Tour routes and a replay feature for recent matches.

November 24, 2026Source: Nintendo resmi haber sayfası
Mario Kart World
Update

Mario Kart World's 1.9.0 update released on November 24, 2026, for the Nintendo Switch 2 system. According to Nintendo's official What's New page, it adds 8 courses and 2 new Knockout Tour routes, along with a replay feature in the menu.

The 8 additions are remixes of existing Mario Kart World courses, including Mario Bros. Circuit 2, DK Pass 2 and Shy Guy Bazaar 2. In Knockout Tour, the Feather Rally route links Mario Bros. Circuit to Boo Cinema, while Lucky Cat Rally connects to Sky-High Sundae. Both unlock after clearing at least one Knockout Tour rally. The new replay feature saves your most recent races and battles, and when the storage limit is exceeded, the oldest replays are deleted first.

For players, the most noticeable change is the chance to try these remixed layouts and rewatch recent matches from the menu. Nintendo also says more Knockout Tour routes are planned for future updates.
