Pokémon Pokopia's Apples to Applin event runs until December 1

An apple-themed event brings Applin and its evolutions to Pokémon Pokopia towns on Switch 2.

Nintendo has announced the Apples to Applin event for Pokémon Pokopia on Nintendo Switch 2. The event begins on November 16 and runs until December 1. Players start by talking to Applin near a Pokémon Center and befriending it. Next, they collect Syrupy Apples from special apple trees and trade them at a Pokémon Center for apple-themed items. Those items let players build habitats that attract Flapple, Appletun, Dipplin and Hydrapple. To open the event, the system software must be updated to Ver. 2.0.1. The game should be launched while connected to the internet, and the Switch 2 date and time must match real time. The event does not take place in Cloud Islands or Bubbly Basin. For multiplayer, players should use Palette Town.
