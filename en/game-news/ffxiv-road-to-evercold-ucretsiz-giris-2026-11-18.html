Final Fantasy XIV offers up to 30 days of free play

The Road to Evercold promotion has begun.

November 18, 2026Source: Steam resmi haber
FFXIV
Image: SteamFree

The Road to Evercold promotion for Final Fantasy XIV has begun, preparing players for the upcoming release of Evercold. According to the official Steam announcement, this is the longest Free Login Campaign the game has ever run, letting players play for up to 30 days at no cost. The campaign runs from 12:00 a.m. PST on November 16, 2026 to 6:59 a.m. PST on January 14, 2027. Players who are not eligible for the free login campaign but have kept supporting the game will receive a selection of items through the Moogle delivery service, provided they log in before maintenance for Patch 8.0 begins. A limited-time Tales of Adventure: Winter's Prelude is also available at a special price, and players who have completed the Endwalker main scenario can use it to jump straight to Patch 7.5. The package stays available until Patch 8.0 is released. A new Letter from the Producer by Producer and Director Naoki Yoshida also marks the start of the promotion.
