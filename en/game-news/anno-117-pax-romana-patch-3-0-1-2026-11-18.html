Anno 117: Pax Romana Patch 3.0.1 is live

Harbour and trade changes arrive in Aegyptus.

Patch 3.0.1 for Anno 117: Pax Romana went live on November 18, 2026, at 10 AM UTC (11 AM CET), according to the developer announcement on Steam. Three islands in the far north of Aegyptus gained extra coastal space for buildings that need a harbour, which should make them more useful as trade hubs between Aegyptus and other provinces. Granary bonuses no longer stack: only the buff from the highest-level Granary in an area applies. Heron in Aegyptus now requests Scribe Sets instead of Wax Tablets. On the fix side, the patch addresses frame rate drops on PlayStation 5 when viewing large settlements, which could fall to single-digit frames per second, and a crash caused by running out of memory on consoles, especially PS5. A crash on some PCs with Intel Arc graphics cards, in the main menu or when loading a save, is also fixed. Known issues remain: some groundplates, wall systems and the Oasis Overgrowth may be invisible in the game, and DLSS Frame Generation can cause FPS drops and visual distortions. The developer advises players not to place ornaments in their harbour for now.
