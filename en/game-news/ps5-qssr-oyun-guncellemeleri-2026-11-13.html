PS5 QSSR: Spider-Man 2 update is live, with more games to follow

PlayStation confirms separate rollout windows for the next QSSR game updates.

November 13, 2026Source: PlayStation Blog
PlayStation 5
Image: PlayStation BlogUpdate

PlayStation's November 13 announcement confirms that Marvel's Spider-Man 2 has its PS5 QSSR update. Control Resonant is scheduled for December, with No Man's Sky following in the coming weeks. The Finals, Arc Raiders and Hell Let Loose: Vietnam also have updates ahead. We recommend updating Spider-Man 2 and judging the image on your own display. For the other titles, follow their individual patch notes before expecting the same support.
