Wuthering Waves adds a Fullpackage download mode on Steam

Steam players can now download and launch the full package directly through Steam.

November 18, 2026Source: Steam resmi haber sayfası
WuWa
Image: SteamUpdate

Wuthering Waves now has a second download mode on Steam, called Fullpackage, alongside the existing Default mode that runs through the launcher. The official Steam announcement says the Steam download method switched to launcher mode in Version 3.8. Some players, however, preferred downloading and starting the game directly through Steam, so the development team added this option. In Fullpackage mode the complete package downloads through Steam, and the game starts from the launch option “Play Wuthering Waves - fullpackage”. Only the default High-Definition package is offered in this mode. The Default mode lets players choose graphics quality and resource tiers based on their device and disk space. Game content is identical in both modes, and account data is fully shared. To switch, right-click the game in your Library, open Properties, and choose Fullpackage under Game Versions & Betas. Switching modes can overwrite files from the other mode, and launcher downloads may stay on the drive, so Steam recommends deleting them manually after switching.
