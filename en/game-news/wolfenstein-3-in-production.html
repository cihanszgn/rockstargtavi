Wolfenstein 3 officially announced, in production at MachineGames

November 12, 2026

At Xbox's 25th anniversary event, Wolfenstein 3 was confirmed to be in development at MachineGames.

Xbox announced during its 25th anniversary celebration that Wolfenstein 3 is in production at MachineGames. Xbox CEO Asha Sharma made the announcement, and the studio confirmed that Brian Bloom and Debra Wilson are returning as BJ Blazkowicz and Grace Walker. No release date or platforms were given in the announcement.

Source: Xbox Wire, November 12, 2026 · Xbox Wire, November 12, 2026
