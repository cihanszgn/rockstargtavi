Dungeonbound 1.0.14 adds Jerry and new anti-cheat

Steam's official announcement says the update adds the hero Jerry, a slot machine shrine, server-side cheat detection and balance changes.

November 22, 2026Source: Steam resmî haber sayfası
Dungeonbound
Image: SteamUpdate

Dungeonbound version 1.0.14 went live on Steam on November 22, 2026. According to the official Steam news post, the update adds a new hero, new quests, a new shrine and stronger anti-cheat measures.

The new hero Jerry unlocks after you beat 25 Jerry's. The new Slot Machine shrine lets you put a weapon in and spin; three of a kind blesses it. New Map, Settings and Report Bug buttons sit next to the battle speed control, and a new REPORT RIVAL button targets boards that look impossible.

On the cheating side, edited saves and boards are now caught and kept off the leaderboards and out of the rival pool. Server-side detection has been fine-tuned, and a trust system is in place to ban cheater builds from appearing as ghosts. Balance changes give small buffs to Ashigaru, Relic Hunter, Snuffy, Jeiku and the Archer, plus buffs to common weapons and to rarely picked items (+30).

In co-op, the lobby host now picks the act and the Depth, and everyone sees their own damage breakdown after a battle. Killing Death now ends the run and posts your score. Quitting and resuming no longer undoes or repeats shrines and chests, and a new device can no longer overwrite your Steam Cloud save.
