WoW: Forever hotfixes: Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza and an Action Bar fix

Blizzard's November 13, 2026 hotfix list covers the weekly fishing tournament and an Action Bar bug.

According to Blizzard's November 13, 2026 WoW: Forever hotfix notes, many more players can now win in each ruleset of the weekly Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza tournament. The notes say work on performance continues. A bug that reset the Action Bar for Secondary Talents has also been fixed.
