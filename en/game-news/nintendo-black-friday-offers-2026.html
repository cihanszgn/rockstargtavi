Nintendo Black Friday: Switch 2 + Mario Kart World bundle at $499.99

November 12, 2026

Digital game sales start November 19, with the Mario Kart World bundle and physical game deals from November 22 (US).

Nintendo revealed its US Black Friday offers. A sale on select digital games runs from November 19 through December 2 at 11:59 p.m. PT. From November 22 through December 5, the Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World Bundle, with a 3-month Switch Online Individual membership, sells for $499.99, a $50 saving. From November 22 to 29, select games such as Donkey Kong Bananza are $20 off. Offers are available while supplies last.

Source: Nintendo News, November 12, 2026
