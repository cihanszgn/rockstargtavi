Zenless Zone Zero Version 3.4 launches December 2 with free S-Rank Robin

The Finality of the Day adds Void Hunter Sunbringer, a Honkai: Star Rail crossover, free Robin, and Bangboo Pom-Pom.

HoYoverse has officially announced that Zenless Zone Zero Version 3.4, titled "The Finality of the Day," launches on December 2. The update introduces Sunbringer, a new Void Hunter and the game’s first Enchainer Agent, alongside new story content, outfits, and Signal Search reruns. Headlining the patch is a Zenless Zone Zero and Honkai: Star Rail crossover: players who log in during the event period can claim the limited-time S-Rank Agent Robin for free along with her upgrade materials and a custom set of Drive Discs with recommended Main Stats. Fellow crossover Agent Kafka will join the limited-time Signal Search after January 1, while the "Lost in a Theatrical Dream" event awards the new Bangboo Pom-Pom for free. Check-in events grant 10 Encrypted Master Tapes, 10 Boopons, 1,840 Polychrome from the version check-in, and 480 Polychrome from "The Final Battle Before Dawn." The Special Program redemption code expires November 22 at 23:59:59 (UTC+8).
