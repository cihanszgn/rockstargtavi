WARDOGS warns against NVIDIA drivers 621.14, 621.24, 621.35

The developer advises skipping NVIDIA drivers 621.14, 621.24 and 621.35 and rolling back to 617.42 if the game stutters.

November 23, 2026Source: Steam geliştirici duyurusu
WARDOGS
Image: SteamUpdate

The WARDOGS team says some NVIDIA driver versions are causing problems in the game, according to a Steam announcement published on November 23. Until NVIDIA ships a fix, the developers recommend avoiding drivers 621.14, 621.24 and 621.35.

Players have reported freezes, shader compilation problems and stuttering since 621.14. The issue affects some RTX 40 and 50 series owners, but not everyone. Driver 621.14 was released on November 4, 621.24 on November 12, and 621.35, a hotfix, on November 20; the hotfix does not resolve the problem. The team adds that this is not isolated to WARDOGS, as numerous other PC titles show the same behaviour on these drivers.

The developer says 617.42 is the last driver it knows to be stable for WARDOGS. If you are on an affected driver and the game runs fine, you do not need to change anything. If you get freezes or stuttering, rolling back to 617.42 is the first thing to try. The team is watching NVIDIA's releases and will say when a newer driver is safe.
