Halo: Campaign Evolved is getting Firefight in a free update

November 12, 2026

The four-player co-op PvE mode Firefight arrives this winter as a free update.

Halo Studios announced that Firefight, the four-player cooperative PvE survival mode, is coming to Halo: Campaign Evolved this winter in a free update. Players team up to hold the line against wave after wave of enemies. Attendees at Xbox FanFest in Seattle were the first to try it on the evening of November 12, with new maps and plenty of weapons. More details will be shared at Halo Fest on December 12-13 in Seattle.

Source: Xbox Wire, November 12, 2026
