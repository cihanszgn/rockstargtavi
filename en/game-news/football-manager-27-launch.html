Football Manager 27 is out now, including with Game Pass

November 10, 2026

FM27 launched on Xbox on PC and FM27 Console on Xbox Series X|S, both available with Game Pass.

Sports Interactive confirmed that Football Manager 27 launched on November 10. FM27 is out for Xbox on PC and FM27 Console for Xbox Series X|S, and both are available with Game Pass. The game promises greater control over your tactical vision and a deeper football world to explore. The Xbox Wire post also highlights five new additions.

Source: Xbox Wire, November 10, 2026
