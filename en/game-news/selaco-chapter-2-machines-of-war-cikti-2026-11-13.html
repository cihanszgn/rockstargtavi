Selaco Chapter 2: The Machines of War is out with 19 new maps

The Early Access shooter's biggest update adds a 6–8 hour campaign, but old saves will not carry over.

Selaco's developer released Chapter 2: The Machines of War on Steam on November 13, calling it the largest update of the game's Early Access period. According to the announcement, it adds 19 new maps and roughly 6–8 hours of campaign, and players who finished Chapter 1 can jump straight into the new levels. Enemy AI has been rebuilt, and the update introduces heavy weapons such as miniguns and rocket launchers that cannot be stowed, a jetpack, reworked slide and dash moves, and a companion system with Follow and Hold orders. Mutators can now be changed mid-campaign in Safe Rooms, and there are 16 new Steam achievements. The important catch is that existing saves are not compatible with this version. Anyone still partway through the original campaign can select the 'chapter1' branch in Steam's Betas tab to finish it on the old build. The developer also announced a 33% Steam discount lasting two weeks and plans a Special Campaign-focused update for the first quarter of 2027. Our advice: decide which branch you want before updating if you have an unfinished run, and check the store page for the current price.
