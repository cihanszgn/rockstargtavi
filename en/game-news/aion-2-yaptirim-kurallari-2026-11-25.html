AION 2 changes sanctions: unbound Kinah deductions

Steam's official notice adds currency deductions and a debt rule that restricts transfers and trading until a negative Kinah balance is repaid.

November 25, 2026Source: Steam resmî duyurusu
AION 2
Image: SteamUpdate

According to the official notice on Steam, AION 2's operational policy has been revised, and its sanctions now include a Currency Deduction. The notice gives two effective dates: November 25, 2026 in the introduction and December 18, 2026 in the detailed section.

Currency Deduction recovers in-game currency obtained through rule violations, and it applies even when a temporary game ban or another access restriction is imposed. If the balance is too low, it can go negative by the missing amount. Under the new Unbound Kinah Deduction Policy, that shortfall becomes an outstanding debt. While it remains, the character cannot transfer servers or use Unbound Kinah. On the Exchange, listing items and claiming sales proceeds are restricted, and on the Marketplace, listing, claiming proceeds and purchasing are also restricted.

The notice also expands the rules for real-money transactions. Items obtained that way can be recovered, and in-game currency can be recovered or deducted. If real-money trading is repeated for profit, a Permanent Game Ban may apply even on the first sanction. Players who disagree can request cancellation of their membership, and if they neither consent nor refuse by the effective date, the changes are considered accepted. Objections go through the Customer Support link in the notice.

For players, the practical takeaway is to review their trading habits now, especially on the Exchange and Marketplace, and to keep the effective date in mind.
