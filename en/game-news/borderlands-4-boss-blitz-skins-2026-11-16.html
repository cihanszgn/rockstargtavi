Borderlands 4 community beats 6.2 million bosses, unlocks four skins

Four weapon skins are unlocked as a community reward, redeemable with a SHiFT code until December 31, 2030.

November 16, 2026Source: Steam resmî duyurusu
Borderlands 4
Image: SteamNews

Borderlands 4 players have defeated 6,261,242 bosses in the Boss Blitz Community Challenge, and the official Steam announcement says the reward is four new weapon skins. Troubled Upbringing, Fresh Spray, Tally Them All and Merch Line can be applied to any gun, so every Vault Hunter can use them. Each one is inspired by a base-game Vault Hunter: Troubled Upbringing by Vex, Fresh Spray by Rafa, Tally Them All by Amon and Merch Line by Harlowe. Players redeem the reward with a SHiFT code, either on the Gearbox SHiFT website or from the in-game SHiFT menu. Each code works once per SHiFT account and must be redeemed by December 31, 2030. A copy of Borderlands 4 and a SHiFT account are required. The announcement also says rewards from earlier Community Challenges can still be claimed the same way.
