PUBG update 44.1: PUBG x IVE collaboration and Rondo map changes

Update 44.1 goes live on PC on November 4 and on console on November 12, with the PUBG x IVE collaboration.

According to the PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS 44.1 patch notes, PC maintenance runs on November 4 from 00:00 to 08:30 UTC and console maintenance on November 12 from 01:00 to 09:00 UTC. The update brings the PUBG x IVE collaboration on Rondo, Xeno Point: Hell and "HLB: Dawnbreakers of Rondo". Vehicle durability adjustments and PGC 2026 map themes are also included.
