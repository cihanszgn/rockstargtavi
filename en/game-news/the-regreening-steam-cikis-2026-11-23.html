The Regreening launches December 7 on Steam

Restore a deserted world by growing plants, rebuilding wildlife habitats and upcycling junk, with no timers to rush you.

November 23, 2026Source: Steam mağaza sayfası
The Regreening
Image: SteamLaunch

The Regreening is a cozy soilpunk simulation from developer Positive Impact Games, planned to launch on PC via Steam on December 7, 2026. Gematsu reported the PC launch, and the Steam store page lists the same planned release date.

In the game you restore a deserted world. You grow plants, rebuild wildlife habitats and revive ecosystems, and you can upcycle abandoned junk into tools and decorations. Nature spirits drawn from folklore offer abilities in exchange for your help.

There are no timers, so you progress at your own pace. A free demo is already on Steam, so you can try the game before buying it. Publishing is handled by ByteRockers' Games and Gamirror Games. For players, the practical takeaway is simple: mark December 7 and test the demo to see whether the game suits you.
