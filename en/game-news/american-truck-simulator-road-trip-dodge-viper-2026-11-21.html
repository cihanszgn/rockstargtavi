Dodge Viper GTS and ACR preview for American Truck Simulator DLC

Details on the Viper models in the Road Trip: RAM & Dodge DLC.

The official Steam news post from the American Truck Simulator developers previews the Dodge Viper for the upcoming Road Trip: RAM & Dodge DLC. The DLC features the 2002 Dodge Viper GTS, along with the more track-focused American Club Racer (ACR). Both versions use an 8.0-litre V10 engine, producing 450 horsepower in the GTS and 460 in the ACR, paired with a six-speed manual gearbox. The ACR adds lighter rims, racing seat belts, ACR badges and a choice of black or cognac leather interiors. Exterior colours include Viper Red, Viper Racing Yellow, Graphite Gray, Blue Pearl and Black and Silver, with or without racing stripes. The GTS offers black or cognac leather, plus customisable rear spoilers, badge colours, rear diffusers and hoods. The announcement does not give a release date for the DLC.
