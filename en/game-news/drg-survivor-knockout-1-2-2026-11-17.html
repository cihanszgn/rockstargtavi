Knockout expansion is out

Brawler gets 11 new weapons, a new biome and Hazard 6.

The Knockout expansion and Update 1.2 for Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor are now live. According to the official Steam announcement, the Brawler receives 11 new weapons. The Shockwave Emitter fires wide shockwaves, Diffractor Spikes are plasma-powered throwing spikes, and the Thunderlash is a whip built to take down the toughest enemies. The Brawler's Bonk ability benefits from certain upgrades, including damage and weapon range. The new Sandblasted Corridors biome brings sandstorms that can disrupt your scanners. Hazard 6 is also here: enemies you fail to defeat on one stage follow you into the next. Fossils and Morkite reactor cores return as secondary objectives. Mastering a weapon on Hazard 5 lets you pick it as your starting weapon, while Hazard 6 mastery unlocks the UltraClock artifact. An extra layer of Overtime upgrades rounds out the update.
