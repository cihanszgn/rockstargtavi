Xbox's 25th anniversary magazine is now open to everyone

Xbox's 25th anniversary special magazine is live on Xbox Wire and readable by anyone.

Xbox has published a special magazine marking 25 years of the platform: "XBOX – The 25th Anniversary Magazine". Rather than only looking back, it covers games, hardware and key moments from past and present, told through the teams who build them. As one example, page 38 features Bowerstone, the capital city of Fable. Xbox printed copies and gave them to community members at XBOX FanFest in Seattle the previous week. Since publication, anyone can read the magazine directly on the Xbox Wire page. Readers can switch to full-screen mode using the arrows in the lower right corner of the viewer.
