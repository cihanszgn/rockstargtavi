Knightica is free on the Epic Games Store until November 19

November 12, 2026

Epic's weekly freebie is Knightica until November 19; Alien: Isolation and Mark of the Deep follow on November 19.

Knightica is the current free game on the Epic Games Store and can be claimed until November 19. Next up are Alien: Isolation and Mark of the Deep, both free from November 19 to November 26. Epic gives away new free games every Thursday.
