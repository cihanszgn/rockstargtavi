VRChat holiday gifting event runs until January 4

Gifting unlocks avatar accessories, profile decor and Hype Train rewards, while community goals unlock stickers and crowns.

November 25, 2026Source: Steam resmî duyurusu
VRChat
Image: SteamUpdate

VRChat has brought back its Holiday Gifting Extravaganza, according to a developer post on its Steam news page. The event runs until January 4, 2027, and players earn rewards by sending gifts to friends or anyone else.

Three community milestones give everyone a reward: the Princess of Frost sticker, the Ice Crown and the Iced Out Watch. Individual gifts also unlock items. Any gift earns the Gingerbread Village Profile Decoration, one VRC+ Subscription gift unlocks the Edible Antlers avatar accessory, two unlock Drosselmeyer's Gift, five unlock the Fresh From the Oven warp effect, and ten unlock the Gingerphone. The Hype Train returns in every instance with three tiers: 10 gifts give the Frosty Ski Goggles, 20 give the Hot Cup of Generosity, and 30 give the Cozy Lodge Portal plus 500 VRChat Credits. The Hype Train award can be received only once.

The most generous gifters who complete every task also earn a Baker Extraordinaire Badge and 5,000 bonus credits in January. The post also brings back content from three popular store updates. The event ends on January 4, 2027.
