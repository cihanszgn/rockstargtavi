Marathon's Symbiosis update lands December 8 with new PvE and PvP modes

November 10, 2026

Bungie set the Symbiosis update for December 8, adding new modes, weapons and a new Runner shell.

According to the PlayStation Blog, Marathon's Symbiosis update launches December 8 for all players. It adds new PvE and PvP modes and new weapons, and the post gives first details on the updated Perimeter zone and the new Bishop Runner shell. Marathon is available now on PS5 with full cross-play and cross-save support. Gameplay requires internet access and a paid console subscription.

Source: PlayStation Blog, November 10, 2026
