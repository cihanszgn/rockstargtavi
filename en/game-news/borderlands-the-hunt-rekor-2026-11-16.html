Borderlands community breaks The Hunt donation record

The fundraiser passed the previous record and the matching goal for St. Jude.

According to the official Steam announcement, The Hunt, the community-organized Legendary loot scavenger hunt across Borderlands 2, Borderlands 3 and Borderlands 4, has set a new all-time donation record for St. Jude Children's Research Hospital. Players completed their runs across the three games for the charity. 2K Foundations and Gearbox had pledged to match every dollar donated up to the previous record of $118,995.80. The community surpassed that record as well as the matching goal. The participants of The Hunt: All Aboard finished their loot hunts in all three games. The announcement does not give a new total. The studio thanked the Borderlands Community Fundraising Team and the St. Jude PLAY LIVE team for their support.
