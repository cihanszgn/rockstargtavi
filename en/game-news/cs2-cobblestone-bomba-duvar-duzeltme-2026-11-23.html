Counter-Strike 2 fixes Cobblestone bomb and wall bugs

Valve's Steam announcement says the update fixes unreachable bomb spots, bad boosts and grenades falling through the map.

November 23, 2026Source: Valve Counter-Strike 2 Steam duyurusu
CS2
Image: SteamUpdate

Valve released a set of Cobblestone map fixes for Counter-Strike 2 on November 23, 2026, according to its official Steam announcement. The update targets several gameplay problems on the map.

At Princess Tower, a bomb can no longer be thrown to a spot players cannot reach. On B, a bug that let grenades fall through the world has been fixed. Valve also corrected some bad wall-bang positions, poor boosts and walkable ledges, and closed various gaps in the level to make player movement smoother.

The patch also makes third-person silent reload animations play at the correct speed and includes various stability improvements. For Cobblestone players, the clearest change is that several known broken spots in the map's bomb, grenade and boost areas have been fixed.
