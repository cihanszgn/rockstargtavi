Nintendo eShop adds digital game gifting for family groups

November 9, 2026

From November 16, family group members on Switch and Switch 2 can gift digital games to each other.

Nintendo announced that a gifting feature arrives in the eShop on November 16, letting family group members give each other digital purchases on Nintendo Switch and Switch 2. Eligible games, DLC and upgrade packs can be gifted. The feature needs system version 23.5.0 or later, released on November 9. Some products may not be eligible, supervised accounts cannot send gifts, and the number of gift purchases in a given period is limited.

Source: Nintendo News, November 9, 2026
