Zelda: Ocarina of Time is now playable on Switch 2

Nintendo says the game is available now, exclusively on Switch 2.

November 16, 2026Source: Nintendo
The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time
Image: NintendoLaunch

Nintendo says The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is available now on Nintendo Switch 2. According to the official Nintendo page dated November 16, the game is offered exclusively on Switch 2. The premise is familiar: the evil Ganondorf plots to seize the Triforce from the Sacred Realm and take over the world. Link, the only Kokiri without a fairy, is joined by Navi at the Great Deku Tree's request and sets out to become the Hero of Time. Nintendo describes the release as the legend reborn for a new generation, with completely overhauled visuals, orchestral music, voice acting and expanded cutscenes. Improved controls and modern camera movement are meant to make it an approachable entry point to the series. The rating notes Animated Blood and Fantasy Violence. The page does not list a price.
