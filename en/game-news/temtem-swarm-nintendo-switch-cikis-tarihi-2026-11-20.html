Temtem: Swarm launches on Nintendo Switch on January 21, 2027 with cross-play

Temtem: Swarm arrives on Nintendo Switch on January 21, 2027 with day-one cross-play across PC and PlayStation plus an all-platform update.

November 20, 2026Source: Steam Resmî Duyurusu (Crema)
Temtem: Swarm
Image: SteamLaunch

In an official Steam announcement on November 20, 2026, the developers of Temtem: Swarm confirmed that the bullet-heaven action game will launch on Nintendo Switch on January 21, 2027. Players can already add the title to their wishlist on the Nintendo eShop ahead of release. The game lets players battle swarms of enemy Tems solo, with friends, or alongside Tamers from around the world.

According to the announcement, the Nintendo Switch edition will feature cross-play from day one, connecting Switch players with communities on PC and PlayStation. Alongside the Switch launch, a new update will roll out across all platforms, introducing memory improvements and bug fixes designed to keep hectic, screen-filling runs running smoothly on every system.
