When is GTA VI online coming? What Rockstar has said and not said

Rockstar and Take-Two have made no official announcement about an online mode, and no date has been given.

Two days before launch, there is no official announcement from Rockstar or Take-Two about a GTA VI online mode. No name, no date and no window has been given.

What Rockstar has said: Take-Two's press release of June 24, 2026 announces the game for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026 at $79.99 and describes a single-player experience. Take-Two's fiscal 2027 second-quarter results release repeats the release date and platforms and does not mention an online mode.

What is only a rumor: according to PC Gamer, Twitch CEO Dan Clancy implied in September 2026, based on the platform's conversations with Rockstar, that GTA VI online would launch in 2027. That is an implication, not an official confirmation. PC Gamer also notes that Rockstar announced the online parts of GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 about a month before launch, while this time launch week has arrived with no word.

In short, the only officially confirmed thing is the single-player experience; until Rockstar says more, any date or detail for GTA VI online is rumor.
