Marvel Rivals patch brings Incursion Point on November 26

The update adds the Incursion Point map, an 18 vs. 18 Chrono Annihilation mode, new bundles and a Black Friday sale of up to 40% off.

November 26, 2026Source: Steam resmî duyuru
Marvel Rivals
Image: SteamUpdate

Marvel Rivals' November 26, 2026 patch adds the new Incursion Point map and an 18 vs. 18 Chrono Annihilation mode. According to the official Steam announcement, the patch is scheduled to drop at 09:00 UTC on November 26, 2026, with no server downtime.

In the story, Doom 2099 sends his full armada into the present timeline to annihilate one Earth, and the new map puts players in the middle of that all-out invasion. Incursion Point will not be available on PS4 due to hardware performance limitations. New store bundles include Lady Loki - Envious Emerald, Scarlet Witch - Virtual Insanity and Moon Knight - Moonjack Hacker, with new chromas for Deadpool - Moment Stealer and Cloak & Dagger - Gilded Agate. The bundles are available until December 25, 2026 at 02:00 UTC.

The Black Friday Sale runs for a limited time with discounts of up to 40% off. Purchases fill a progress bar that unlocks a serial-numbered Black Friday 2026 Nameplate, Units and an exclusive Title. Rivals' Day 2026 also opens the Galactic Center hub, where Ghostworks Arcade offers an extra 400 Units and the Arcade Ace Title to players who collect Arcade Stamps.

For players, the main takeaways are the new 18 vs. 18 map, the cosmetic bundles and discounts of up to 40% during the Black Friday Sale. Players on PS4 will miss the new map, so check your platform before queuing.
