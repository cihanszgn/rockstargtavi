Hounded comes to PS5 in 2027

Duke the Border Collie leads a search for his missing master.

November 17, 2026Source: PlayStation Blog (resmî duyuru)
Hounded
Image: PlayStation Blog (resmî duyuru)News

Hounded is coming to PS5 in 2027, according to the official PlayStation Blog announcement. Developed by Imprnt Studios and Silver Lining Interactive, the game puts players in the paws of Duke, a young Border Collie searching for his missing master, Artemys Fletcher. The story unfolds in Anglodia, a flintlock fantasy world loosely inspired by late 18th-century Britain, where an oppressive regime is tearing the land apart. The team has spent just over two years on the project and built a handcrafted Motion Matching system to animate Duke's movement, with every motion and expression made by hand. Duke has abilities tied to The Tide: the Power Bark breaks through obstacles, the Power Howl charges mysterious technology, and Imprints let him see echoes of moments that happened just before he arrived. A developer diary is now available, and the game can be added to PS5 wishlists today.
