Football Manager 27 update 27.1.0 is set to roll out this week

The update brings crash fixes, faster loading on lower-end PCs and Match Engine tweaks.

November 16, 2026Source: Steam
FM27
Image: SteamUpdate

The official Steam announcement for Football Manager 27 says update 27.1.0 will roll out later this week. Since the game launched on November 10, the team has said it is focusing on community feedback and on reports logged in its bug tracker. The update will reach FM27, FM27 Console and FM27 Touch players, and a separate update for FM27 Mobile is also due this week.

The most direct change for players is technical. The announcement lists numerous crash fixes, shorter loading times on lower-end PCs when larger databases are loaded, and several interface performance improvements. The Information Display setting can now be chosen by anyone with a screen resolution of 1080p or higher. A new slider also makes attribute borders transparent.

On the pitch, the Match Engine receives adjustments to passing, dribbling and pressing decisions. The developer notes that these changes need thorough testing, so it is worth watching how they change your own matches once the patch is live.
