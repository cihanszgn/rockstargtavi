The Blood of Dawnwalker is 20% off on Steam

The game is discounted on Steam for the first time until December 7, and Update 1.2 adds New Game+ with a level cap of 100.

November 24, 2026Source: Steam resmî duyurusu
The Blood of Dawnwalker
Image: SteamDeal

The Blood of Dawnwalker is on sale on Steam for the first time. The game is 20% off until December 7, according to an announcement posted on its Steam news page on November 24.

The same announcement says the game is nominated for Best Narrative and Best Role Playing Game at The Game Awards, with voting open at thegameawards.com/nominees. Update 1.2 with New Game+ is also live: players can replay the story with their level, skills and inventory. The level cap rises to 100, there is no zone scaling, and new NG+ gear comes with upgraded stats.

If you have not picked the game up yet, the discount runs until December 7. If the story stayed with you, you can also nominate it for Outstanding Story-Rich Game in the Steam Awards 2026. The announcement lists no regional prices, so check the Steam store page for the current price in your region.
