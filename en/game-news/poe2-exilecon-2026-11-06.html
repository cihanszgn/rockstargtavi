Path of Exile 2 becomes free-to-play on December 11

According to the ExileCon 2026 announcements, Acts 5 and 6, the Shadow and Duelist classes and a new league arrive too.

According to Grinding Gear Games' ExileCon 2026 announcements, Path of Exile 2 becomes free-to-play on December 11 (PST). The same update brings Acts 5 and 6, the Shadow and Duelist classes and the "Conquerors of Wraeclast" league.
