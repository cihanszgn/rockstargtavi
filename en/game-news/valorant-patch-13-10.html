VALORANT Patch 13.10: replay bookmarks, new assist rules, Gauntlet: Glitched

Patch 13.10 brings replay bookmarking, changes to assist rules and Gauntlet: Glitched updates.

According to Riot Games' 13.10 patch notes, replays can now be bookmarked. A damage assist requires dealing more than 10 damage within 3 seconds of a teammate's kill, while a healing assist only counts if the healed player gets a kill within 10 seconds. Gauntlet: Glitched gets updates for Cypher, Skye and Hush.
