DREADMOOR Early Access Update 7 is live with save and quest fixes

Fixes for save loading, quest progression, fishing and boat controls.

DREADMOOR's seventh Early Access update is now live. The developer says it focuses on save and load reliability, quest progression, player interactions and fishing, with fixes for boat controls, tutorials and the interface. Players who loaded a save could find tools and quest items impossible to pick up, the crane-collapse sequence could replay, and the boat could stay locked after loading a save from the first quest. Saving could also remain blocked after cutscenes, combat or other situations, and docking points in hubs now keep their state. The Escape quest could get stuck when saved during the water monster sequence, and a skippable trigger in the Hook Gun sequence has been fixed. On the boat, the compass orientation and the gamepad camera at the control panel were corrected. Two new fish were added to the game files, but they are not in the world yet and cannot be caught. Bug reports can be sent through Steam Discussions or the official Discord.
