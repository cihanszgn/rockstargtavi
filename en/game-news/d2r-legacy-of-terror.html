Diablo 30th anniversary event Legacy of Terror starts in Diablo II: Resurrected

The event runs from November 13 to 19, 2026 and launches the Season 16 Ladder.

November 13, 2026Source: Blizzard News
Diablo
Image: Blizzard NewsUpdate

According to Blizzard, the Diablo 30th anniversary event Legacy of Terror runs in Diablo II: Resurrected from November 13, 5:00 PM PST to November 19, 5:00 PM PST. The Season 16 Ladder launches during this window. The event brings a guaranteed Diablo Clone every 6 hours in Hell difficulty and Terrorized Zones in every zone after Baal.
