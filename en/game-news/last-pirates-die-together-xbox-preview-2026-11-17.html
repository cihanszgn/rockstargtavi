Last Pirates: Die Together lands on Xbox Game Preview

On Xbox Series X|S and PC, with Steam crossplay.

RetroStyle Games' online co-op comedy horror game Last Pirates: Die Together launched on November 17 in Xbox Game Preview on Xbox Series X|S and PC. The studio, based in Kyiv, Ukraine, says lobbies can mix console and Steam players. Up to four players sail to a cursed island, grab treasure with stretchy arms and haul it back to the ship before the captain's quota runs out. The game includes a Mimic monster that copies a player's look and borrows short lines from voice chat. Xbox Play Anywhere means one purchase covers both Xbox Series X|S and PC. As a Game Preview title, the game is still a work in progress and may change. The studio says Xbox will receive the same updates as Steam at the same time, with a new event due this winter before the full release.
