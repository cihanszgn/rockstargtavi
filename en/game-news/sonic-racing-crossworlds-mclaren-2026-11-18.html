Sonic (McLaren Racing) arrives in Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds

The McLaren Racing Festival starts November 19.

November 18, 2026Source: Steam resmî duyuru
Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds
Image: SteamUpdate

The Sonic (McLaren Racing) skin and the McLaren Racing Car machine are now available in Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds, according to the official Steam announcement. An internet connection is required to claim the add-ons.

The McLaren Racing Festival runs alongside the release, from November 19, 2026 at 7:00 PM ET to November 29, 2026 at 6:59 PM ET. Players earn Festival Points during the event to unlock exclusive items.

In the top team, the player with the most Rule Points wins the Blue Crown. Festival rewards are tied to your save data: deleting your save also deletes them, and they cannot be earned again.
