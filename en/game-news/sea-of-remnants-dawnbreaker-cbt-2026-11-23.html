Sea of Remnants hits Xbox Series X closed beta on December 3

The free-to-play turn-based roguelike opens pre-download on December 1 for Xbox Insiders, and the test ends December 18.

November 23, 2026Source: Xbox Wire
Sea of Remnants
Image: Xbox WireNews

Sea of Remnants is a free-to-play, turn-based roguelike adventure from Joker Studio, and its Dawnbreaker Closed Beta Test arrives on XBOX Series X on December 3, 2026. According to Xbox Wire, the test is aimed at XBOX Insiders, and it costs nothing to join and welcomes players worldwide.

Pre-download begins December 1, 2026, and the test runs until December 18, 2026. Test progress is wiped when it ends, but Dawnbreaker participants get an exclusive commemorative reward that carries over to the global launch. The beta opens the White Sea and Bridge Sea regions, adds Journey and Roaming quests, and lets players try two ships: the Drift Bottle and the Fireworks Wanderer. XBOX Series S support is planned for global launch.

There are two ways in. A limited number of direct registration spots open on launch day, first come, first served, subject to server capacity. Players can also enter the additional access draw by pre-registering or signing up for the CBT with a NetEase Passport account. Signing up does not guarantee access. During the test, multiplayer is limited to selected casual minigames on the same server, and the game is still in development.

For players, the real question is whether a free test is worth their time. Anyone who joins early helps shape the game and keeps the commemorative reward, while beta progress itself will be reset. Xbox Insiders can find the download steps in the Xbox Insider Hub app under Previews.
