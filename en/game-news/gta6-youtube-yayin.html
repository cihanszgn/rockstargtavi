YouTube hosts a 24-hour launch livestream

November 12, 2026

The pre-show starts at 11 PM ET on November 18 and it ends live from Miami.

According to the YouTube Blog, youtube.com/youtube will run a 24-hour stream on launch day with live gameplay, creator podcasts and original segments. A pre-show starts at 11 PM ET on November 18, the main stream begins at 12 AM ET on November 19 and it wraps with live coverage from Miami.

Source: YouTube Blog, November 12, 2026
