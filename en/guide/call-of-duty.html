Call of Duty in 2026: Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone

#

Call of Duty is a first-person shooter series with a new main game roughly every year. The current main game is Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, made by Infinity Ward. According to official announcements it launched on 23 October 2026 for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Battle.net, Steam and Xbox on PC) and Nintendo Switch 2; there is no PS4 or Xbox One version. It contains a campaign, Multiplayer and the returning DMZ mode.

Warzone is the free-to-play battle royale (last one standing wins) side. With Season 1, which began on 6 November 2026, Warzone became fully integrated with Modern Warfare 4. The new Battle Royale map Hajin and the new Resurgence map Zodiac arrived; Rebirth Island returns, and Verdansk comes back later in Season 1.

Tip

This guide covers the lasting basics of the main game and Warzone. Which weapon is best changes from season to season; check the official patch notes for the current picture.

Before you start: platform, install, security requirements

#

You buy the main game from the store page; the store offers Standard and Vault editions, and Vault adds extra operator packs (for example Hostile Alliance: Price, Valeria, Ghost, Blix). Warzone, on the other hand, is free separately. On PC, Modern Warfare 4 officially asks for the following.

  • Minimum: 64-bit Windows 10, Ryzen 5 1400 or Core i5-6600, 8 GB RAM, Radeon RX 470, GeForce GTX 970 or GTX 1060, or Arc A580; 115 GB of SSD space.
  • Recommended: Windows 11, Ryzen 5 3600 or Core i7-8700K, 16 GB RAM, Radeon RX 6600 XT, GeForce RTX 3060 or Arc B580; again 115 GB of SSD space (Steam page).
  • Security requirement: PC needs TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot. According to Activision this generally means Intel 8th generation or newer or Ryzen 2000 and up, UEFI boot, a GPT disk and Secure Boot enabled.

Watch out

Without TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot the game will not launch on PC. Before changing BIOS settings, read the steps on Activision's support page; a wrong setting can stop your computer from starting.

Tip

The install needs 115 GB of SSD space. Check your free space before downloading; if you want to try Warzone first, you can buy the main game later.

Your first matches, step by step

#
  1. 01
    Start with Warzone or core Multiplayer modes

    Warzone is free; Multiplayer teaches weapons and maps in shorter matches. Try both and see which you like.

  2. 02
    Build a loadout

    Pick a main weapon, a sidearm, equipment and Perks. At first start with a ready-made class, then change things one by one.

  3. 03
    Learn the map's flow

    Every map has paths players use often. When you die, note where you were hit from and do not take that route again.

  4. 04
    Land safely in Warzone

    Instead of jumping into busy areas, land at an edge point, find a weapon and armor, and follow the direction of the circle.

  5. 05
    Fix one thing after every match

    Find out why you died: were you exposed, did you not recover, were you alone? Fix that in the next match.

Tip

In your first week of Multiplayer, ignore your K/D ratio. Aim to play consistently: learn to disengage and reposition before you die.

The gentlest path for day one: start with Resurgence in Warzone (you can come back after dying) or use short Multiplayer modes to practice movement and aiming.

Multiplayer, Warzone and DMZ: the differences

#

The three modes are parts of the same game but their rules differ. Think about your class and your goal according to these differences.

TopicMultiplayerWarzoneDMZ
Match goalShort team matches, a target score depending on the modeBe the last one standing on a big map; in Resurgence, come back after dyingComplete missions and extract from the map
Where your weapon comes fromThe class you build in Create-a-Class; a primary and a secondary weaponWeapons you find on the ground and the free Loadout Drop that lands for your squadYou can enter with two primaries, two secondaries or one of each; more with a Weapon Backpack
CostIncluded with the main gameFreeInside Modern Warfare 4 (with the main game)

About Zombies: no Zombies mode appears in the official Modern Warfare 4 announcements (launch, Season 1 and Warzone). It is officially not announced; if it is, you will hear it on the Call of Duty blog.

Tip

Do not use the same class in every mode. Speed and close fighting pay off in Multiplayer; range and armor matter in Warzone; in DMZ, getting out alive matters as much as what you carry.

Weapon classes, Gunsmith and building a class

#

Create-a-Class unlocks at Player Level 4. According to the official launch post, primary weapons come in six classes; the numbers below are the launch counts, while the roles are general game knowledge.

ClassCountGeneral role
Assault rifle8Balanced at most ranges; the easiest start for a beginner
Submachine gun (SMG)5Close range and mobile play
Shotgun2Very close range; strong in tight spaces
Light machine gun (LMG)3Big magazine; sustained fire and holding an area
Marksman rifle3Mid and long range; every shot counts
Sniper rifle3Longest range; demands patience and position
Secondary weapon8A fast switch when the primary runs dry
  • Attachments are shared within a class. According to the official announcement, attachments in Gunsmith are now shared by weapon class, which makes building and experimenting easier.
  • Apex Attachments. These are separate attachments unlocked by fully leveling a weapon; they do not use an attachment slot and can noticeably change how the weapon behaves.
  • Gunny. A ready-made build helper for newcomers: it assembles close-, mid- or long-range builds, designed by Infinity Ward's weapons team, from the attachments you have unlocked. It is optional and you can edit the result by hand.
  1. 01
    Open Create-a-Class

    It appears in the menu at Level 4. First pick a ready-made class and look at what it contains.

  2. 02
    Pick the primary weapon

    Choose one of the classes in the table and write the class's purpose in a single sentence.

  3. 03
    Start from Gunny, then edit

    Generate Gunny's close, mid or long-range suggestion; change attachments one by one and feel the difference in a match.

  4. 04
    Choose a secondary or Overkill

    In Multiplayer, equipping the Overkill Perk lets you carry a second primary in place of your secondary. You find it in the first Perk group, unlocked at Level 4.

  5. 05
    Pick three Perks

    In Multiplayer, 18 Perks are split into three groups and you pick one from each (Pick 3). Choose Perks to match the class's purpose.

  6. 06
    Finish with equipment

    Fill in Tactical, Lethal, Field Upgrade and Killstreak choices in turn.

Tip

Try each weapon build for at least five matches. A build's weakness usually shows over several matches, not one.

Core mechanics: loadouts, Perks, position

#

Weapons change from season to season, but these four habits do not.

  • Perks: Warzone has 18 Perks in total. The first fifteen are chosen into three slots before a match; the remaining three are Elite Perks found during a match, and only one can be equipped at a time. According to the official post, Elite Perks add a fourth slot and are found at Buy Stations or through Supply Run Contracts.
  • Position: corners and door frames help in close fights; staying in motion in open areas gives an opponent little time.
  • Armor and healing: in Warzone, put on armor plates before a fight; the Tempered Perk cuts plate refills from three plates to two (Season 1 post).
  • Teamwork and comms: pings and short callouts beat long talk. Details like buy-back cost and the Wingman Perk in Resurgence change with the season.

Tip

Instead of piling Perks onto a class, write the class's purpose in one sentence: close quarters or mid-range? Choose Perks to match that purpose.

Warzone: the Gulag, redeploying and Drop Kits

#

In Warzone, dying is not always the end of the game. According to the Season 1 patch notes, Hajin has a new Gulag (the duel arena you enter when you die) and Resurgence gained new consumable items called Drop Kits.

Drop KitWhat it gives when you redeploy
InsuredYou redeploy faster and keep all your Cash
ArmoredYou land with full armor, bonus armor plates and a Self-Revive Kit
LoadoutYou land with your current Loadout
RadarYou land with an active Radar Ping, a UAV and a Supply Sweep
  • Drop Kit rules: found only in Resurgence modes, they affect only the Operator who equips them, are consumed on redeploy, and only one can be equipped at a time; picking up another replaces the current one.
  • The Gulag: Hajin's new Gulag comes in two arena variants; you can mantle over obstacles, spectate from ground level at certain angles, and local weather affects the environment.
  • Redeploying in Battle Royale: players who are bought back redeploy with the weapon tied to that circle's Gulag loadout.
  • Contracts and extra items: in Circle 3, drones carrying Mobile Buy Stations enter the map; shooting them down gives access to powerful gear found nowhere else (Fly Buy). A free Loadout Drop also lands for your squad. In the Resurgence event Bounty Contest, every squad is automatically assigned a Bounty Contract.

Tip

Before you die in Resurgence, check which Drop Kit you carry. The Armored kit is for a fast recovery, the Loadout kit for carrying on with the same weapon; choose by your goal.

Settings: solid starting values

#

The following are starting suggestions; they vary with your hardware and habits. Change only one setting at a time.

  • Field of view (FOV): start around 100. Too high shrinks targets, too low hides your surroundings.
  • Frame limit: match your monitor's refresh rate and keep VSync off; a steady rate beats sudden spikes.
  • Sensitivity: start at a middle value and tune your aim-down-sights sensitivity separately; change them one at a time.
  • Audio: play with headphones and choose a sound profile that emphasizes footsteps.
  • Keyboard and mouse: start with a mouse DPI between 400 and 1600; find a sensitivity where you can comfortably turn around in half a swipe. Put lean, slide and jump on keys your fingers reach easily.
  • Nintendo Switch 2: according to the Beta announcement, Joy-Con 2 mouse controls, gyro, the Switch 2 Pro Controller and handheld play are supported; gyro aiming is handy for fine adjustment.
  • Controller: raise dead zones just enough to stop drift, and do not turn off aim assist; see the in-game setting descriptions for details.

Tip

Test each change in a match; if results are not better, go back to the old value. Changing everything in one evening hides what actually works.

Progression: the Battle Pass and the season

#

According to the official announcement, the Season 1 Battle Pass offers over 100 rewards across 100 tiers. There is a catch-up system for players who arrive late: as the season goes on, rewards are brought forward automatically for you even if you joined late. On price, the Warzone Season 1 post says 1,100 COD Points for the Battle Pass and 2,400 COD Points for the Battle Pass Bundle; what COD Points cost in money varies by platform, and the official post gives no single Turkish lira price.

  • Before buying the pass, review the free reward track; see in advance whether the premium rewards are worth it for you.
  • New event modes arrive mid-season (for example Rockets and Snipers and Extreme Resurgence were announced); plan your progress around them.
  • Prestige: at max Soldier Rank you choose between two paths. Classic Prestige relocks Create-a-Class content but gives faster XP and exclusive rewards; Regular Prestige restarts at Level 1 without relocking your loadouts.
  • You can use the main game and Warzone on the same account; Warzone became integrated with the main game in Season 1.

Tip

Do the math before buying: if you think you cannot finish before the season ends, do not buy. The catch-up system helps, but it is not unlimited.

Common beginner mistakes

#
  • Taking the same route again and again. If an opponent camps, pick a different route.
  • Fighting without armor. In Warzone a fight without plates is often a lost fight.
  • A class with no equipment. With only a weapon you are in danger; do not neglect equipment and tactical gear.
  • Entering a crowd alone. Going into an area without teammates makes you an easy target.
  • Finding out about security requirements afterward. On PC, check TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot before you buy.
  • Constantly chasing new weapons. Do not judge a weapon before using it for a few days; feel takes time.

Tip

Play twenty matches with the same weapon, then decide. Constantly switching hides which mistakes come from the weapon and which from you.

Account security and spending warning

#

The main game is paid and Warzone is free; both allow extra purchases through Battle Passes, bundles and COD Points. Before buying, read what is permanent and what is seasonal, and set a monthly limit.

  • Add two-step verification to your account and do not reuse your password on other sites.
  • For children, turn on your platform's parental controls (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Steam or Battle.net) and require approval for spending.
  • Stay away from links and files promising free COD Points or secret weapons; they are used to steal accounts.

Watch out

Stay away from cheat software, aimbots, macros or special input devices, buying or selling accounts or items, and having someone boost your rank. The PC security requirements are strict for this reason; a banned account may be lost permanently, and these offers are often scams.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is Warzone free?

Yes, Warzone is free to play. The main game (Modern Warfare 4) is bought separately.

Can I play on PS4 or Xbox One?

Modern Warfare 4 did not launch for PS4 or Xbox One; it launched for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Nintendo Switch 2.

Why won't the game start on PC?

The most common cause is TPM 2.0 or Secure Boot being off. Follow the steps on Activision's support page.

Can I try Warzone before buying the main game?

Yes, Warzone is free. If you do not like it, you lose nothing.

Do I have to buy the Battle Pass?

No. There is a free reward track; the premium part is optional.

Do Warzone and Modern Warfare 4 share progression?

According to the official announcement, from Season 1 Warzone integrates Modern Warfare 4 content and seasonal progression.

Can I play the game in Turkish?

Turkish is not in the Steam language table; it does not appear among the languages listed for interface, audio and subtitles.
