Call of Duty in 2026: Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone

#

Call of Duty is a first-person shooter series with a new main game roughly every year. The current main game is Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, made by Infinity Ward. According to official announcements it launched on 23 October 2026 for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Battle.net, Steam and Xbox on PC) and Nintendo Switch 2; there is no PS4 or Xbox One version. It contains a campaign, Multiplayer and the returning DMZ mode.

Warzone is the free-to-play battle royale (last one standing wins) side. With Season 1, which began on 6 November 2026, Warzone became fully integrated with Modern Warfare 4. The new Battle Royale map Hajin and the new Resurgence map Zodiac arrived; Rebirth Island returns, and Verdansk comes back later in Season 1.

Tip

This guide covers the lasting basics of the main game and Warzone. Which weapon is best changes from season to season; check the official patch notes for the current picture.

Before you start: platform, install, security requirements

#

You buy the main game from the store page; the store offers Standard and Vault editions, and the Vault list shows BlackCell (1 season) and extra packs. Warzone, on the other hand, is free separately. On PC, Modern Warfare 4 officially asks for the following.

  • Minimum: 64-bit Windows 10, Ryzen 5 1400 or Core i5-6600, 8 GB RAM, Radeon RX 470, GeForce GTX 970 or GTX 1060, or Arc A580; 115 GB of SSD space.
  • Recommended (90 frames at High in Multiplayer): Windows 11, Ryzen 5 3600 or Core i7-8700K, 16 GB RAM, Radeon RX 6600 XT, GeForce RTX 3060 Ti or Arc B580, 8 GB of video memory.
  • Security requirement: PC needs TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot. According to Activision this generally means Intel 8th generation or newer or Ryzen 2000 and up, UEFI boot, a GPT disk and Secure Boot enabled.

Watch out

Without TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot the game will not launch on PC. Before changing BIOS settings, read the steps on Activision's support page; a wrong setting can stop your computer from starting.

Tip

The install needs 115 GB of SSD space. Check your free space before downloading; if you want to try Warzone first, you can buy the main game later.

Your first matches, step by step

#
  1. 01
    Start with Warzone or core Multiplayer modes

    Warzone is free; Multiplayer teaches weapons and maps in shorter matches. Try both and see which you like.

  2. 02
    Build a loadout

    Pick a main weapon, a sidearm, equipment and Perks. At first start with a ready-made class, then change things one by one.

  3. 03
    Learn the map's flow

    Every map has paths players use often. When you die, note where you were hit from and do not take that route again.

  4. 04
    Land safely in Warzone

    Instead of jumping into busy areas, land at an edge point, find a weapon and armor, and follow the direction of the circle.

  5. 05
    Fix one thing after every match

    Find out why you died: were you exposed, did you not recover, were you alone? Fix that in the next match.

Tip

In your first week of Multiplayer, ignore your K/D ratio. Aim to play consistently: learn to disengage and reposition before you die.

Core mechanics: loadouts, Perks, position

#

Weapons change from season to season, but these four habits do not.

  • Perks: Warzone has 18 Perks in total. The first fifteen are chosen into three slots before a match; the remaining three are Elite Perks found during a match, and only one can be equipped at a time. According to the official post, Elite Perks add a fourth slot and are found at Buy Stations or through Supply Run Contracts.
  • Position: corners and door frames help in close fights; staying in motion in open areas gives an opponent little time.
  • Armor and healing: in Warzone, put on armor plates before a fight; the Tempered Perk cuts plate refills from three plates to two (Season 1 post).
  • Teamwork and comms: pings and short callouts beat long talk. Details like buy-back cost and the Wingman Perk in Resurgence change with the season.

Tip

Instead of piling Perks onto a class, write the class's purpose in one sentence: close quarters or mid-range? Choose Perks to match that purpose.

Settings: solid starting values

#

The following are starting suggestions (my own experience); they vary with your hardware and habits. Change only one setting at a time.

  • Field of view (FOV): start around 100. Too high shrinks targets, too low hides your surroundings.
  • Frame limit: match your monitor's refresh rate and keep VSync off; a steady rate beats sudden spikes.
  • Sensitivity: start at a middle value and tune your aim-down-sights sensitivity separately; change them one at a time.
  • Audio: play with headphones and choose a sound profile that emphasizes footsteps.
  • Controller: raise dead zones just enough to stop drift, and do not turn off aim assist; see the in-game setting descriptions for details.

Tip

Test each change in a match; if results are not better, go back to the old value. Changing everything in one evening hides what actually works.

Progression: the Battle Pass and the season

#

According to the official announcement, the Season 1 Battle Pass offers over 100 rewards across 100 tiers. There is a catch-up system for players who arrive late: as the season goes on, rewards are brought forward automatically for you even if you joined late. BlackCell is the premium tier with extra perks.

  • Before buying the pass, review the free reward track; see in advance whether the premium rewards are worth it for you.
  • New event modes arrive mid-season (for example Rockets and Snipers and Extreme Resurgence were announced); plan your progress around them.
  • You can use the main game and Warzone on the same account; Warzone became integrated with the main game in Season 1.

Tip

Do the math before buying: if you think you cannot finish before the season ends, do not buy. The catch-up system helps, but it is not unlimited.

Common beginner mistakes

#
  • Taking the same route again and again. If an opponent camps, pick a different route.
  • Fighting without armor. In Warzone a fight without plates is often a lost fight.
  • A class with no equipment. With only a weapon you are in danger; do not neglect equipment and tactical gear.
  • Entering a crowd alone. Going into an area without teammates makes you an easy target.
  • Finding out about security requirements afterward. On PC, check TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot before you buy.
  • Constantly chasing new weapons. Do not judge a weapon before using it for a few days; feel takes time.

Tip

Play twenty matches with the same weapon, then decide. Constantly switching hides which mistakes come from the weapon and which from you.

Account security and spending warning

#

The main game is paid and Warzone is free; both allow extra purchases through Battle Passes, bundles and COD Points. Before buying, read what is permanent and what is seasonal, and set a monthly limit.

  • Add two-step verification to your account and do not reuse your password on other sites.
  • For children, turn on your platform's parental controls (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Steam or Battle.net) and require approval for spending.
  • Stay away from links and files promising free COD Points or secret weapons; they are used to steal accounts.

Watch out

Stay away from cheat software, aimbots, macros or special input devices, buying or selling accounts or items, and having someone boost your rank. The PC security requirements are strict for this reason; a banned account may be lost permanently, and these offers are often scams.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is Warzone free?

Yes, Warzone is free to play. The main game (Modern Warfare 4) is bought separately.

Can I play on PS4 or Xbox One?

Modern Warfare 4 did not launch for PS4 or Xbox One; it launched for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Nintendo Switch 2.

Why won't the game start on PC?

The most common cause is TPM 2.0 or Secure Boot being off. Follow the steps on Activision's support page.

Can I try Warzone before buying the main game?

Yes, Warzone is free. If you do not like it, you lose nothing.

Do I have to buy the Battle Pass?

No. There is a free reward track; the premium part is optional.

Do my weapons disappear in a new season?

Check the official season announcements and the in-game progression page; I did not verify this detail for this guide.
