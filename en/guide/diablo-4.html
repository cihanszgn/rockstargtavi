Where Diablo 4 stands in 2026

#

Diablo 4 is a loot-driven action role-playing game in which you slay monsters and collect gear in a dark world. The story takes place in the land of Sanctuary; the real fun is finding the combinations of gear and skills that make your character stronger.

The game runs in seasons. Season 15, Hell's Legacy began on 15 September 2026 (patch 3.2.1, with the series' 30th anniversary and a Witcher crossover). The big change was the Lord of Hatred expansion (28 April 2026): two new classes, reworked skill trees for all classes, a new Loot Filter, the return of the Horadric Cube, War Plans (a structured endgame route), the level cap rising from 60 to 70 and Torment 1-12 difficulty tiers. A test realm (PTR) for Season 16 ran at the end of October, but its release date has not been announced; do not make dates up.

Tip

When a season changes, characters reset and everyone starts from the same line, which makes it the best entry point for a new player. Your season characters move to the permanent (Eternal) side at season's end.

Before you start: which edition to buy

#

Diablo 4 needs a Battle.net account and a constant internet connection; the game is online even when you play alone. On PC you buy it from Battle.net or Steam, on consoles from the Xbox and PlayStation stores. The Xbox page also mentions Game Pass; coverage can change, so check in the store. For the Battle.net version on PC you need the Battle.net desktop app. Cross-platform progress is tied to the account, but a purchase is separate in each store, so decide up front which platform you will play on.

SituationWhat to buyNote
You own nothingAge of Hatred CollectionBase game, Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred together
You own the base gameLord of HatredIncludes Vessel of Hatred too
You want more cosmeticsDeluxe or Ultimate editionAdds Platinum and cosmetics; does not speed up gameplay
Multiple platformsA separate purchase per platformPurchases are tied to the platform

Buying the bundle up front is usually cheaper than buying expansions later. Lord of Hatred costs US$49.99 (Game Informer); the Turkish lira price has not been officially announced, so see the exact amount in your own store. Lord of Hatred requires the base game. The system requirements table is in the next section.

Watch out

Buy the game only from Blizzard's Battle.net store, Steam, and the Xbox and PlayStation stores. Unknown sites selling cheap keys mostly sell codes bought with stolen cards, which can get your account suspended.

The first hours: step by step

#

A new player's first goal is to finish the campaign and reach level 70 and the door to the Torment difficulties. The campaign takes you to roughly level 40-50; endgame activities fill in the rest.

  1. I
    Pick your class

    Look at the class section below and pick what suits your taste. Your character name cannot be changed later, but you can try another class on another character.

  2. II
    Play the campaign

    Follow the main quests; side quests and exploring give you levels, items and gold. As you open regions, fast-travel points open too.

  3. III
    Compare new items

    Wear a new piece if it has a higher main stat (for example weapon damage or armor) and extra properties that help you than your current piece. Sell or salvage useless items.

  4. IV
    Spend skill points

    Spend points in the skill tree; every class starts with a strong main attack. You can refund points you dislike for gold.

  5. V
    Get stronger quickly

    As you level up, new skills unlock; build a small setup: one main attack, one movement skill, one defense.

  6. VI
    Move to the post-campaign route

    Once you reach level 70, turn to endgame activities such as War Plans and the Pit.

Tip

Turn on the Loot Filter from day one. It hides items that do not fit your class and level, saving both screen clutter and time.

Class choice: a summary of the 8 classes

#

The classes differ a lot in play style. If you do not like one class, it is fine to try another in the next season. Short summary: For newcomers, classes that deal damage from range or summon helpers are generally more forgiving. Spiritborn arrived on 8 October 2024 with Vessel of Hatred, and Paladin and Warlock on 28 April 2026 with Lord of Hatred. Paladin plays with four oaths and Arbiter Form; Warlock uses hellfire, summoned demons and eldritch magic. To play the expansion classes you must own the matching expansion.

Tip

Which class is strongest changes from season to season. The lasting principle: if you are having fun you are on the right class; looking at meta lists after your first character is enough.

Up to level 70 and beyond: the progression path

#
  • Campaign: finish the story; you reach roughly level 40-50.
  • Until level 70: gain levels by playing endgame activities and dungeons; review your new skill tree points.
  • Pit: the dungeon that unlocks at level 70; Torment 1 opens at Pit 10, you move to the next Torment every 5 Pit levels (up to T5), then every 10 levels up to T12.
  • War Plans: a structured path of five endgame activities; a good guide for a player who does not know which activity to do when.
  • Horadric Cube: used to transform and upgrade items.

At every stage your goal is to build enough damage and toughness to move to a higher difficulty. If you are progressing too slowly, gather gear on a lower difficulty and come back; if you are progressing fast, try a higher one. At level 70 there is no cap showing the character's power; progress is measured by item quality and Pit level. To set a weekly goal, note your previous Pit level and try to climb one step in each session. If you get stuck three times in a row on one step, check the resistance and armor values of your gear before you change your build.

Items, gold and resource management

#
  • Item rarity: rare (yellow) and legendary (orange) items are usually the strongest; blue and white items serve temporarily in the early game.
  • Toughness: armor and resistances matter as much as damage on high difficulties; focusing only on hit power gets you killed.
  • Gold: it is spent on skill respecs, item upgrades and craftsmen; do not be afraid to spend plenty in the early hours.
  • Storage: if your inventory is full, use the stash and the shared chest.
  • Account-wide progress: some progress is tied to the account; you do not have to start from scratch on a new character.

Simple rule: if an item does not make you stronger, do not carry it. But a legendary piece's power is sometimes specific to a build; before throwing away an item you are unsure of, consider salvaging it or putting it in storage. In the item list, lock a piece before you salvage anything; an item salvaged by mistake does not come back. Because gold keeps flowing, set yourself a small budget rule: vital resistance and armor first, then damage properties. Clean out the surplus in your stash at regular intervals, because once the space is full you have no room for a new legendary item.

Torment tiers: how difficulty unlocks

#

After level 70, difficulty selection opens through the dungeon called the Pit. Torment 1 is locked at Pit 10; later tiers open as you reach certain Pit levels. The Pit levels below are compiled from community sources and may change between seasons.

TierPit level that unlocks itNote

Watch out

As you jump tiers, monsters get tougher and your armor and resistance take penalties. If your deaths climb, return to a lower tier and fill out your defensive stats; the reward is for surviving, not for growing.

Seasons, the Seasonal and Eternal realms

#

Diablo 4 renews itself with seasons. The Season Journey is a chain of goals and rewards completed through the season, and the battle pass comes along with it. The game has two realms: Seasonal carries the season content, the journey and the battle pass, and needs a new or reborn character. The Eternal realm has no season content and never resets.

FeatureSeasonal realmEternal realm
Season content and journeyYesNo
Battle passYesNo
CharacterNew or arrived through RebirthPermanent characters
ResetItems move to the Eternal stash at season endNever resets
  1. I
    Wait for the season start

    At the start of a new season, create a Seasonal character; everyone starts from the same line.

  2. II
    Open the season journey

    Look at the season goals in the in-game menu; you earn rewards as you complete them.

  3. III
    Use Rebirth

    Rebirth, added in Season 15, converts an Eternal character to Seasonal at level 1. Class, name, looks, Hardcore state, cosmetics, mounts, pets and playtime are kept; gear and combat progress are not.

  4. IV
    Carry over at season end

    Seasonal items can be moved to the Eternal stash until the end of the following season.

Tip

If you cannot choose between Seasonal and Eternal, start with Seasonal; you learn the new season mechanic along with every other player.

Endgame activities and item upgrading

#

War Plans is a path that lines up to five activities one after another like a playlist. The activities below can enter the list:

Kurast UndercityCrowd clearing of monstersQuick levels and gold

There are two systems for improving an item later: tempering and masterworking. Tempering adds a limited number of new properties to a piece; masterworking strengthens existing properties step by step. Details (the number of steps, the costs, the extra-property pool) change with seasons; see Maxroll or the official patch notes for the current table. The Horadric Cube helps convert items in this chain.

Tip

Before you start upgrading, be sure you will really use an item; tempering and masterworking spend resources and a wrong choice may not be reversible.

Common beginner mistakes

#
  • Wearing items without comparing them, or the opposite, never changing them.
  • Forgetting defense: a damage-only build dies fast on high difficulty.
  • Hoarding gold and never spending it.
  • Chasing the strongest-class list every season and staying on a class you do not enjoy.
  • Rushing to buy Platinum with real money. Platinum is for cosmetics only and does not speed up the game.

Watch out

Buying Platinum does not raise your power in the game; it is only for appearance items. When you think you need to pay for something, stop and think once.

Account security and spending warning

#

Your Blizzard account is valuable because it connects many games. Do not share your account details or security codes with anyone.

  • Turn on two-step verification on your Battle.net account.
  • Cheats, bots, auto-play software and buying or selling items or accounts for real money (RMT) break the game rules and can get your account closed.
  • Links promising free Platinum or items are fake Battle.net login pages; they steal your account.
  • Do not deal with people who buy or sell accounts; it is forbidden and carries a scam risk.

Watch out

Sites giving free Platinum, gold or items are scams. A real gift never asks you for a password or security code.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is Diablo 4 free?

No, it is a paid game. A new player starts with the Age of Hatred Collection, the best-value route.

Can I play alone?

Yes, but the game is online; even solo play needs an internet connection and a Battle.net account.

Do I have to buy Lord of Hatred?

As a new player, buying the Age of Hatred Collection is recommended; level 70, the new classes and the endgame content come with the expansion.

When is Season 16?

Not announced yet. The test realm ran at the end of October; follow Blizzard's announcements for a date.

Which class should I start with?

If a class description excites you, pick it. The class list changes from season to season; fun is lasting.

Can I get stronger with real money?

No; Platinum is only for appearance items.
