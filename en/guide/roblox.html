What is Roblox and what changed in 2026?

#

Roblox is not a single game; it is a platform where you enter small and large games made by millions of users (Roblox calls them experiences). With the same account you can play a survival game one day and a racing or role-play game the next.

The biggest 2026 change was on the safety side. Since 7 January 2026 chat requires an age check worldwide: a facial age estimate through the phone camera, with ID verification optional for players 13 and over. In early June 2026 two child-focused account types also arrived: Roblox Kids (ages 5-8) and Roblox Select (ages 9-15). Note for Turkey: access to Roblox from Turkey was restricted on 7 August 2024. Reports appeared in June 2026 that the restriction was lifted, but another news site wrote that BTK records still showed the block; confirm the current status in official announcements. Using a VPN to get around the block is not recommended.

Tip

If the age is entered wrongly, chat and content limits are applied wrongly too. When you create a child's account, enter their real birth year.

Before you start: account and device

#

Roblox is free and runs on PC, Mac, iOS, Android, Xbox and PlayStation. When you sign up your age group is set, and chat and content settings follow from it:

Age groupAccount type / statusKey point
5-8Roblox KidsOnly games rated Moderate or lower
9-12Roblox SelectRestricted content; screen time and spending controls available
13-15Roblox SelectCannot play Restricted games; parental settings apply
16-17Standard accountFewer parental controls
18 and overAdult accountFull access; extra features with ID verification

Under 9, chat is off by default and only turns on if a parent consents. Roblox classes ages as U9, 9-12, 13-15, 16-17, 18-20 and 21+. System requirements are low; a recent phone or a mid-range computer runs most games, though heavy games may need lower settings.

Watch out

Download Roblox only from the official Roblox site or the app stores. Fake apps and sites offering free Robux steal accounts.

The first hours: step by step

#

The order below gives a safe and efficient start for both child and adult new players.

  1. 1
    Create the account correctly

    Enter the real birth year, choose a strong unique password and turn on two-step verification if possible.

  2. 2
    Finish the age check

    If you want to use chat, finish the facial age estimate. If you skip it chat stays off, which for children is often a good default.

  3. 3
    Play with the avatar

    Edit the look and clothes in the Avatar tab using free options. You do not need to buy paid items at the start.

  4. 4
    Pick a popular, rated game

    On the Discover page read the game description, content rating and player count. Start with a game friends or family already know.

  5. 5
    Learn the controls and sound

    In the first minutes try the camera, movement and jump keys; every game's interface is a little different.

  6. 6
    Set the time

    Decide playing hours up front; parental controls can set a screen-time limit.

Tip

Play together for the first few days. It is the easiest way to learn which games your child likes and who they talk to.

Choosing games and content ratings

#
  • Roblox experiences have a content rating; Kids accounts can play Moderate or lower, and Select accounts cannot enter Restricted games.
  • Look at the like ratio, player count and last update date; games not updated for years often have bugs and empty servers.
  • Avoid games that send you to another site or app or ask for personal information.
  • Once in a server, check the chat and voice settings; talking to the wrong people is often a bigger risk than the game itself.
  • If you want to make a game yourself, try the free Roblox Studio program; the building world is a separate and instructive discovery.

Tip

If a game has an unclear rating or reviews, try it yourself for a few minutes before opening it to your child.

Robux, Premium and spending habits

#

Robux is Roblox's in-game currency, used for clothes, in-game power and extra content. Robux is bought with real money; according to Roblox's announcement, buying through the website, a computer or a gift card usually gives more Robux than buying through a store app (the rate can change, so check the amount shown on screen when you buy).

  • Set a monthly budget and buy Robux for a goal (for example a single item); small repeated purchases add up surprisingly fast.
  • Using a gift card is safer than linking a saved card to the account; the budget limits itself.
  • Premium gives Robux every month; Roblox Plus gives discounts and extra perks. Both are subscription spending; skip them if you do not need them.
  • Free Robux promised by any site, app or video is a scam; no legal free Robux code asks for your password.

Watch out

Sites promising free Robux, free clothes tricks or Robux duplication exist to steal account details. Never share your password, account link or security code with anyone.

Parental controls: where and what you can set

#

After linking the parent account to the child's account, the settings gather in the parental controls section inside Settings. Roblox can rename menus, so check the screen yourself.

  • Content maturity: decide which maturity level of games the child may play.
  • Chat: can be set to on or off; it is off by default under 9.
  • Screen time: a daily limit can be set on Kids and age 9-12 Select accounts.
  • Spending: a monthly spending limit and spending notifications can be set; redeeming a gift card is not counted against this limit.
  • Robux transfers: there are transfer limits, approvals and history records.
  • Trusted friends and inventory visibility: restrict who they can talk to and who can see their inventory.
  • Parent mode: protected by a 6-digit PIN so the child cannot change settings.

Watch out

From age 13 the child can manage their own screen time and blocking, and from 16 up there is even less control. Update settings together with the growing child through conversation.

Linking a parent account: step by step

#

Parental controls are managed through a parent account linked to the child's account. To create a linked parent account, the parent must show they are 18 or older through an age check (Roblox Help, parental controls page). Roblox made the age check mandatory for chat worldwide (January 2026); accounts without an age check can enter limited games and cannot communicate. There are new account types too: Roblox Kids is for ages 5-8 and Roblox Select for ages 9-15. A child moves from Kids to Select at 9 and to a standard account at 16 (Roblox newsroom, April 2026). Always check which account type it is and what it unlocks for the child's age.

  1. 1
    The child signs in

    The child logs into their own account and goes to Settings.

  2. 2
    Add a parent

    Open Parental Controls in Settings, press Add parent and enter the parent's email address.

  3. 3
    Open the email

    The parent opens the message in their own email and chooses to log in with their existing account.

  4. 4
    Verify your age

    Show you are 18 or over with a government ID or credit card, and in some regions with facial age estimation.

  5. 5
    Confirm the link

    Accept the account link; the settings are now managed from the parent account.

SettingWhat it doesAge check needed?
CommunicationDecides who the child can chat withYes for many options
Studio collaborationChooses who they can build with in Studio: All age groups, Similar age groups or trusted friends, Similar age groups, No oneYes for all except No one
Gameplay coordinationCoordinates with players of all ages using short ready-made phrases (Quick Words)No
Sending and getting RobuxYounger users need parent approval for every transferUsers 18+ can transfer without approval
Facial and ID verificationYou choose whether ID verification is allowed for the childYou manage the setting

Tip

Once the link is set up, look through the settings with your child. Explaining the reason for a setting makes it easier for them to accept the rule.

Roblox Plus, Premium and Robux: the differences

#

Roblox Plus launched globally on 30 April 2026 at US$4.99 a month. It offers perks only and includes no monthly Robux. Premium is the older subscription that gives Robux every month. Optional bundles with 500, 1000 or 2000 Robux can be added to Plus (Plus 500, Plus 1000, Plus 2000). The official Premium price varies by region and tier and shows on Roblox's purchase screen; check that screen for the exact amount.

FeatureRoblox PlusPremium
Monthly RobuxNone (available with optional bundles)Yes
Discount on in-game and avatar items10-20%None
Free and unlimited private serversYesNo
Send Robux for freeYesNo
Trading and resaleYesYes

These are subscriptions, so they renew automatically every month. If a child's account has a subscription, the parent should know the renewal date and the cancellation route. If the amount on the purchase screen conflicts with the current table on Roblox's help page, the help page is the one that applies.

Watch out

Subscriptions renew on their own. If you see an unexpected renewal, first check the subscriptions section of the app store; cancelling in the Roblox account may not cancel the store subscription.

Below are examples of long-popular genres; the list always changes, so still check the content rating yourself. Give your child this rule: nobody at Roblox will ask for your password, your code or your address on an off-platform messaging app.

An introduction to Roblox Studio

#

Roblox Studio is the free development program where you make your own experience; it runs on Windows and Mac. According to Roblox's announcement, the Studio interface supports Turkish (Roblox Developer Forum). Everything you make in Studio can be tried on your computer for free; you only need to follow the content rules when you publish and open it to others. Earning money (selling for Robux) has its own rules and often an age condition; see the Roblox Creator Hub for the current terms.

  1. 1
    Install Studio

    Download Roblox Studio from Roblox's official page and log in with your account.

  2. 2
    Pick a template

    Open one of the starter templates; a template with a ready map is easier than an empty one.

  3. 3
    Place objects

    Put parts in the scene and change their size and color in the Properties window.

  4. 4
    Write a small script

    In Luau, the Lua-based language, write a script that changes a part's color when touched; this is your first script.

  5. 5
    Test and publish

    Try it with the Play button, then publish the experience and show your friends.

Tip

Whether a child can build with friends in Studio depends on the Studio collaboration option in the parental settings. You can keep it restricted at first so they learn on their own.

Common mistakes

#
  • Leaving a saved card linked to a child's account. A gift card and a monthly limit are much safer.
  • Leaving chat fully open and never asking who the child talks to.
  • Entering the wrong age. Limits then work for the wrong age group.
  • Accepting a stranger's request to move to a messaging app outside the game.
  • Trading real money or accounts for items. Roblox rules forbid account trading.

Watch out

If someone they do not know asks them to move to an outside app or to share photos or personal details, the child should stop talking and tell an adult; parents can block and report the person.

Account security and spending warning

#

Protecting your account is half of in-game safety. Keep the account's email and phone number up to date so you can recover it if there is a problem.

  • Use a different password for every service and turn on two-step verification.
  • If someone in a game asks for a password, code or cookie in exchange for free items, do not answer; it is a scam.
  • Cheats, exploits, third-party cheating software and account selling both break the rules and get your account banned.
  • Keep spending notifications on; if you see an unexpected purchase, change the password and tell Roblox support.

Watch out

Sites giving free Robux or free membership actually steal accounts and aim at your password. Refuse every Robux offer outside Roblox's official way.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is Roblox free?

Yes, games are free to play. Robux and Premium membership are optional.

What age should my child start?

There are Roblox Kids accounts for ages 5-8; starting together with parental controls is best.

Why is an age check needed for chat?

Since 7 January 2026 Roblox limits chat by age group, so children cannot freely write with adults.

Can I get free Robux?

No. Sites and codes giving away free Robux are scams; buy only from the official store.

How do I limit spending?

Set a monthly spending limit in parental controls and, if possible, use a gift card instead of a saved card.

What if my account is stolen?

Change the password, turn on two-step verification, check your email and open an account recovery request through Roblox support.
