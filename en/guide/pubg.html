What the game is and where it stands in 2026

#

PUBG: Battlegrounds is a battle royale (last one standing wins) where dozens of players parachute onto a large map, scavenge weapons, armor and supplies, and try to be the last survivor. As the match goes on the safe zone shrinks and everyone is pushed toward the same area. The game is free to play, and the PC version can be downloaded from its Epic Games Store page.

As of November 2026 the game is on update 44.1. According to the official patch notes, this version brought the IVE collaboration on Rondo, a new event mode and the Season 44 Ranked rewards; PC maintenance was on 4 November and the console update on 12 November.

According to the official overview, up to 100 players join a match, with Solo, Duo and Squad options. Care Packages dropped from the air and vehicles found on the map are core parts of the game. If you want to try weapons and movement before a real match, the official overview also mentions Camp Jackal, a training area.

Tip

This guide covers lasting principles. Weapon balance and map rotation change with every update, so check the official patch notes for the current state.

Before you start: install and requirements

#

The official system requirements are listed below. If your PC does not meet at least the minimum, the game will stutter; aim for the recommended level for smooth play.

  • Minimum: 64-bit Windows 10, Core i5-4430 or FX-6300, 8 GB RAM, GeForce GTX 960 2 GB or Radeon R7 370, DirectX 11, 40 GB of free space.
  • Recommended: Core i5-6600K or Ryzen 5 1600, 16 GB RAM, GeForce GTX 1060 3 GB or Radeon RX 580 4 GB, 50 GB of free space.
  • Account: on PC you can install through the Epic Games Store page; the game is free.
  • Competitive tier: the official support page also lists a competitive tier: Core i9-9900K or Ryzen 7 3800X, 32 GB RAM, GeForce RTX 2060 Super or Radeon RX 5700.
  • Crossplay: according to the official matchmaking letter, Steam and Epic Games Store players play together on PC, and Xbox and PlayStation players play together on console. Whether PC and console meet in the same match is officially not announced.
  • Console: since 13 November 2025 PUBG Console is supported only on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S (official console service notice).
  • Turkish: the Steam page lists a Turkish interface, the Epic page lists Turkish as a language, and Turkish is also in PUBG Mobile's language list.

Tip

Updates need room. Keep your free disk space a little above the recommended figure, or you will be clearing files at every patch.

Your first matches, step by step

#
  1. A1
    Start with an unranked match

    Stay out of Ranked at first. Mistakes in a normal match do not affect your rank, and you learn the map and how weapons feel.

  2. A2
    Land away from the crowds

    Look at the plane's route and pick a spot away from big settlements, near the edge of the path. Everyone jumps on the same large buildings, and dying in the first seconds teaches nothing.

  3. A3
    Gear up in the first two minutes

    Pick up a weapon, ammo, armor, a helmet and healing items. Do not hunt for the perfect gun; being able to fight with what you have is what matters.

  4. A4
    Track the safe zone

    Open the map often and start moving toward the next circle early. Staying outside the zone deals constant damage, and if you rush at the last moment you get chased down.

  5. A5
    Review your death

    After each match ask three questions: where was I shot from, why was I exposed, could I have avoided the fight? Even a short review improves the next match.

Tip

In your first ten matches aim for survival time rather than kill count. As you last longer, kills come on their own.

Landing, the zone and vehicles

#

The result of a match is usually shaped by three decisions in the first five minutes: where you land, how you read the zone and when you take a vehicle. The points below are lasting principles drawn from general play experience; check the official pages for maps and vehicle lists.

  1. A1
    Choose your landing

    Pick a medium-density spot at the edge of the route with at least two buildings and a vehicle nearby. An empty spot risks having no weapon, a crowded one risks an early death.

  2. A2
    Predict the first circle

    When the first safe zone appears, measure your position against it. If you are near the centre waiting is enough; if you are far, find a vehicle early.

  3. A3
    Stay near the zone edge

    Running to the centre exposes you to enemies from every side. At the edge your back is usually safe and you only watch one direction.

  4. A4
    Use vehicles on purpose

    A vehicle moves you fast, but its noise carries far and makes you an open target. Use it for long distances and never drive into a close fight; stop and take cover first.

Tip

The official maps page lists each map's notable vehicles and features (for example the Erangel Ferry and UAZ on Erangel). For which vehicle types exist on which map, see that page and the official vehicles page.

Tip

Weapon balance changes with every update. Focus on each class's role rather than "best weapon" lists, and pick fights that suit what you hold.

Core mechanics

#

The core of the game rests on a few principles. Once they are solid you play well regardless of which weapon is currently strong.

  • Position and height: high, covered ground is an advantage. A player standing in the open is the easiest target.
  • Sound: footsteps, gunfire and vehicles reveal where enemies are. Good headphones are the cheapest upgrade.
  • Armor and helmet: higher-level gear absorbs more damage. Have your healing items ready before a lethal fight.
  • Zone management: moving early, crossing safely by vehicle and positioning at the zone edge beats arriving late and being chased.
  • Teamwork: give short, clear information with pings. Abandoning a downed teammate often loses you both.

Tip

Before every fight have an exit plan: where do you fall back to, and who covers you? A fight without a plan is a gamble.

Settings: solid starting values

#

The values below are starting points; adapt them to your hardware and habits. Rule: change one setting at a time, try a few matches, then decide.

  • Frame limit: set it equal to your monitor's refresh rate. A higher cap only adds heat.
  • Graphics preset: start at Medium. Lower shadows and effects, and pick texture quality according to your graphics card memory.
  • Resolution: stay at your monitor's native resolution; blur makes spotting targets harder.
  • Sensitivity: start near the middle and adjust in small steps of five to ten percent. Changing it daily ruins your muscle memory.
  • Audio: play with headphones, lower music and ambience, and keep footsteps prominent.

Tip

If the frame rate is poor, lower shadows first, then effects, and resolution last. That order costs the least image quality.

Common beginner mistakes

#
  • Taking every fight. Running toward every gunshot makes you a target for a third party. Assess your position first.
  • Lingering outside the zone. Looting while missing the circle means damage and panic.
  • Healing in the open. While using healing items you are vulnerable; step behind a wall or tree first.
  • Ignoring noise. The sound you make running and in vehicles gives you away; slow down when approaching.
  • Tweaking settings constantly. Changing sensitivity after every match stalls your improvement.
  • Leaving the team behind. If you play in a squad, move together and share information.

Tip

If you keep dying in the same place, change your landing spot. For beginners the fastest improvement comes from starting each match at a different, quieter location.

Account security and spending warning

#

Although the game is free, cosmetics, event passes and crates can be purchased. Before deciding, read on the purchase screen what is random and what is fixed.

  • Set a monthly spending limit and stay within it; do not leave your payment method saved.
  • Protect your account with two-step verification and never reuse your password on other sites. Do not trust "free skin" or "free UC" promises from outside the game; enter your login details only on official game and store screens, and check the address before clicking a suspicious link.
  • For children, turn on parental controls in your store's or console's own settings and require approval for spending.
  • Do not trust fake "free skin" links or sites that ask you to log in. Enter your password only on the official login page.

Watch out

Stay away from cheat software, aimbots, macros, buying or selling accounts, and boosting services. The official team regularly publishes ban announcements; these routes lose you your account permanently and are often scams.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is PUBG free?

Yes, the game is free to play. You do not have to spend money; purchases are mostly cosmetic.

Is my PC good enough?

The minimum is 64-bit Windows 10, 8 GB RAM and a GTX 960 class graphics card. For smooth play aim for the recommended list.

Which map should I play first?

There is no required order. Start with whatever the rotation gives you; Erangel is the classic starting map.

When should I enter Ranked?

When you handle the zone, loot and basic fights comfortably in unranked matches. There is no rush.

Is PUBG Mobile the same game?

It is a separate app set in the same franchise: different controls, its own modes and its own update schedule.

Do PC and console players play together?

According to the official matchmaking letter, Steam and Epic play together on PC, and Xbox and PlayStation on console. PC and console meeting is officially not announced.

What if I meet cheaters?

Use the in-game report tool and keep evidence. Do not retaliate yourself; if you use banned software you will be banned too.
