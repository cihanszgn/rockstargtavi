Where the game stands in 2026

#

Rise Online World is an Unreal Engine 5 based online role-playing game developed by Rokosoft (Rokogame Studios), released on 15 April 2022. You download it and register on the official site. The mobile version was also released on 10 April 2026.

The current PC patch is 16.0.3 (6 November 2026). The 16.0.0 patch on 3 November moved the game to Unreal Engine 5.8. There are two active servers: Aarvad and Galantis. Galantis was formed on 16 July 2026 by merging the Galia and Mantis servers.

Rise Online World is known as a classic Korean-style MMORPG: the focus is hunting, levels, gear and mass battles. Most of the information here rests on a Turkish fan guide site and the official site. Because the game gets patches often, follow current patch notes on the official 'patch notes' page.

Tip

The level cap is 85 (the highest zone range and the guide pages point to it). The game is free to download; the revenue model is Premium and Cash purchases.

#

You download the game from the official site. You open your account there and verify your e-mail. The table below is the system requirements published by Rise Rehberi; the page was updated on 15 August 2026, so it predates the Unreal Engine 5.8 switch of 3 November. No new list was published on the official site after that switch, so the values may be out of date.

Before installing, leave enough free disk space and update your graphics driver. The Unreal Engine 5.8 build may prepare shader and lighting data on first launch; if the first launch takes long, do not assume the computer has frozen. On weak hardware, lowering graphics settings in the game improves smoothness.

Watch out

The table comes from a third-party guide site and contains an old Windows list. No problem is expected on today's Windows 10 or 11; still, check storage space and your graphics driver before installing.

The first hours, step by step

#
  1. 1
    Create an account

    Register on the official site, confirm the e-mail and choose a strong password you do not use elsewhere.

  2. 2
    Download the game

    Download and install the client from the official site. Do not download the file from any other source.

  3. 3
    Pick a server

    Choose one of Aarvad and Galantis. If you have friends, join their server; crowding and the economy differ from server to server.

  4. 4
    Choose race and class

    Race is for looks. Choose the class using the information in the next section.

  5. 5
    Allocate stats

    You earn stat points at each level; allocate them according to your class. Read in the in-game window what each stat does.

  6. 6
    Start quests and hunting

    Do the starter quests, hunt in a zone that fits your level and take care not to die; death costs experience.

The aim of the first hours is not fast leveling but getting to know the systems: interface, chat, forming a party and the market. After entering the game, look at sound, graphics and key bindings in the settings window.

A practical note for returning players: if you took a long break, read the patch notes first. Because the 16.0.0 patch changed the game engine, the interface, settings and performance may differ from before. Once in the game, check which server your character stayed on and whether your name or clan information changed because of mergers.

Tip

Before allocating your stats all at once at the start, read the in-game description once; looking at the allocations suggested in class guides reduces the room for error.

Choosing a race and class

#

There are two races: Lunaskar and Protean. According to guide sites the race difference is cosmetic and does not change gameplay. The real decision is the class.

ClassSubtypesIn short
RogueArcher, AssassinFast and mobile; damage from range or up close
WarriorAttacker, DefensiveMelee; attack or defense focused
PriestHealer, Buffer, Debuffer, Battle Priest, INT PriestSupport and healing; some types also attack
MageFire, Ice, LightningMagic damage; different play by element

The stats are Strength, Health, Dexterity, Intelligence and Magic. You earn 3 stat points per level from level 1 to 60 and 5 after level 61.

When choosing a class, also think about how much time you can give the game. Support-heavy classes need a group; for someone who wants to progress alone, a damage-focused type may be more comfortable. These are general tendencies; the exact power balance changes with patches, so look at a current class discussion in the official Discord or community channels.

Tip

If you want the easiest first choice, starting with Warrior is a reasonable pick, but that is a general view; choose the playstyle you like.

Race, class and stat distribution: detailed table

#

Rise Online has four classes and each class has sub-types; the two races (Lunaskar and Protean) do not change gameplay, the difference is only in looks (apart from balance issues in Death Valley and PvP events). The table below combines class, sub-type and the main stat used for damage.

ClassSub-typesMain stat (damage / mana)Shared stat
WarriorAttacker, DefensiveStrengthHealth
RogueArcher, AssassinDexterityHealth
PriestHealer, Buffer, Debuffer; Battle Priest and INT Priest rolesBattle Priest: Strength; INT Priest: IntelligenceHealth
MageFire, Ice, LightningMagic (spell attack), Intelligence (mana)Health

Five stats: Strength, Health, Dexterity, Intelligence and Magic. Strength raises attack power for Warrior and Battle Priest and carrying weight for all classes; Health raises maximum HP for all classes; Dexterity raises the Rogue's attack, Magic the Mage's attack, and Intelligence the mana of Priest and Mage. From level 1 to 60 you gain 3 points per level, from 61 you gain 5.

Bonus trap: on items, 'HP Bonus' raises maximum HP directly, while 'Health Bonus' raises the Health stat; likewise 'MP Bonus' raises mana directly and 'Magic Bonus' raises the Magic stat. Do not mix the two bonuses, their effects are not the same. Each class also has 6 elemental resistances (fire, ice, lightning, poison, curse, holy) and weapon resistances; as resistance rises, damage of that type is reduced.

Tip

Before spending stat points write down your class's main stat and Health; since you will finish the first 60 levels at 3 points each, a wrong distribution is costly.

Leveling and the death penalty

#

Level rises through monster hunting and quests. Rise Rehberi has a table of the EXP needed per level; the figures change with patches, but the cap is 85 and from level 61 you gain 5 stat points per level. To level up, balance quests and monster hunting; progressing by hunting alone is slow, and questing alone can leave you short on gear.

  • Dying costs about 4 percent of experience.
  • Premium lowers this loss: Craft to 0.4 percent, EXP to 0.8 percent, Farm, Battle and Elite to 2 percent.
  • Hunting steadily in a zone that fits your level is often faster than diving into strong monsters.

After allocating your stats for your class, progress with easy monsters in the early levels and keep your potion and death costs low. When your hunting speed starts to fall in a zone, move up to the next one; if deaths rise, go back.

Watch out

The death-penalty rates rest on a third-party guide site and may change with patches. Go by the in-game description.

Professions: gathering and crafting

#

Rise Online has two profession groups and the materials from gathering professions are used in crafting professions. According to Rise Rehberi the gathering professions are Archaeology, Fishing, Harvesting, Herbalism, Mining, Quarrying, Skinning and Woodcutting. The crafting professions are listed as Alchemy, Armor Smithing, Blacksmithing, Carpentry, Cooking, Jewel Crafting, Leatherworking and Stonemasonry.

GroupProfessionsWhat it is for
GatheringArchaeology, Fishing, Harvesting, Herbalism, Mining, Quarrying, Skinning, WoodcuttingRaw material used in crafting
CraftingAlchemy, Armor Smithing, Blacksmithing, Carpentry, Cooking, Jewel Crafting, Leatherworking, StonemasonryTurns raw material into potions, weapons, armor and accessories

Each profession's level thresholds and recipes are not officially stated; learn them from the in-game profession window. A practical start: while leveling, pick a crafting profession matching the potion or gear type your character will use and a gathering profession that supplies it; it also brings extra income in trade.

Premium and purchases: what to know

#

The game has Premium types: Craft, Farm, XP, Battle, Elite and Complete. Each gives an advantage for a different need; for example it reduces the death penalty. Purchases are made through authorized providers.

Premium types reduce the EXP loss after death: Craft lowers it to 0.4%, EXP to 0.8%, Farm, Battle and Elite to 2% (without Premium the loss is 4%). PC Premium prices are not officially stated. The mobile version's prices are given in TL on Rise Rehberi's announcement page and appear in the table below; prices can change over time.

Cash examples: 500 Cash for 93 TL, 1,000 + 20 Cash for 187 TL, 5,000 + 200 Cash for 938 TL, 10,000 + 500 Cash for 1,876 TL. This table belongs to the mobile version; there is no information that PC prices are the same.

Follow a simple rule when deciding on a purchase: first try the game for a while without premium, see where you struggle, and only then consider the type that meets that need. Campaigns and discounts change often; there is no need to rush. Pay only through the authorized provider shown on the official site and keep the receipt.

Watch out

Buying or selling in-game items, gold or accounts for real money with other players (RMT) is forbidden and gets the account closed. Use only the official purchase routes.

Should I buy Premium? Without Premium you lose 4% EXP on death; at high level each death can cost a significant part of a level. So if you will join battle events where you die often, such as Death Valley and Inferno Temple, a Premium type makes sense; if you will play calmly with quests and professions, start without Premium for the first weeks and watch your death count. Check the current price on the official site before deciding; mobile prices may not apply to PC. Do not buy or sell your account, character or Cash with third parties for real money; this both breaks the rules and carries a scam risk.

Events and clans

#

Rise Online World's center of gravity is mass battle events. Known events: Inferno Temple, Crystal Fortress War, Blood Valley, Death Valley and Sevenfold; a community site also lists Mount Race. According to the official patch notes Sevenfold times were added to the in-game Event Calendar, so the official source of event times is the in-game calendar; there is no fixed time list readable from outside (not officially stated).

EventKnown detail (official patch notes)
Inferno TemplePatch note gives minimum entry level 84; teams are mixed from two races; extra crystals by Gear Score
Crystal Fortress WarHas a Surrender system: the event ends by itself when more than half the team confirms
Blood ValleyPatch note lists separate reward points for winner and loser (winner 100, loser 25)
Death ValleyLevel 50-85 map; war between races, balance debates exist
SevenfoldTimes are in the in-game Event Calendar

The clan is at the center of these events. As a new player, joining an active and regular clan makes both learning and joining battles easier. Before joining, learn the clan's rules and expectations.

Before battle events, prepare together with your clan: set a meeting time, assign roles and check equipment. Because event rewards and rules may change with patches, follow official announcements for current information.

Tip

Be prepared in battle events: check your potions, equipment and the event time in advance.

Common mistakes and safety

#
  • Allocating stats without researching.
  • Dying in a zone far above your level and losing experience.
  • Using the same password on other sites.

Watch out

Stay away from cheats, bots, macros, exploits, private servers, account and item selling (RMT) and pirated clients. They lead to account closure and theft of your data. Download the game only from the official site.

The game's staff will not ask for your password. Do not click links that arrive in in-game chat or by message. If you have a question you can ask in official or community channels, but never answer anyone who asks you for a password, a code or money.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is Rise Online World free?

It is free to download; revenue comes from Premium and Cash purchases. Mobile prices are announced in TL; PC prices are not officially stated.

Which server should I pick?

Aarvad and Galantis are the active servers. Picking the server your friends play on is the easiest.

Does race change gameplay?

According to guide sites, the Lunaskar and Protean difference is cosmetic.

What do I lose when I die?

About 4 percent of experience; Premium reduces this rate. Check the current rate in the game.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes, the mobile version launched on 10 April 2026.

What is the current patch?

On PC, 16.0.3 (6 November 2026).

What is the level cap?

The highest map range is 85 so the cap appears to be 85; The official site states no explicit cap.
