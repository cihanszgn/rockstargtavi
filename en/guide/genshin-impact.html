What is Genshin Impact and where does it stand now?

#

Genshin Impact is an open-world action role-playing game by HoYoverse. In a world called Teyvat, while looking for your lost sibling, you explore, solve puzzles, fight enemies and combine elements. The game is free to download and play; the main way to obtain characters and weapons is a luck system called a "wish".

The current version is 7.2 "Hour of Ruin"; it launched on 4 November 2026 and its wish banners run until 24 November. This version brings new 5-star characters Mitya (a catalyst user whose weapon is Starsealed Edict) and Valeriy, plus a new 4-star character. There is also Archon Quest Act V "Hour of Ruin" and Act VI, new Snezhnaya areas Novokitezhgrad and Wingspan Peak, and the event "The Grand Defense". If you are just starting, you do not need to catch up on all of this right away; the game moves you forward step by step.

Tip

Version information refreshes roughly every six weeks. Follow current banners and events through the game's own news screen or the official news page.

Before you start: platform, size and language

#

Genshin Impact is played on PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S (since 20 November 2024), iOS, Android and HarmonyOS NEXT (since 10 September 2025). PS4 support ended on 8 April 2026; the Nintendo Switch version has not been released. The game is free; its income comes from the gacha (luck-based wish) system. An account works across platforms; check the limits of progress sharing in the official statement before you register.

Watch out

Download the game only from the official site, store apps (App Store, Google Play, PlayStation Store) or the official launcher. Third-party "ready-made accounts" and modified client files get your account banned.

The first hours: step by step

#
  1. 1
    Start the story

    Finish the introductory quests; game systems unlock one by one. Instead of rushing, explore the first region (Mondstadt) at ease.

  2. 2
    Use the free wishes

    The beginner banner gives 20 wishes and guarantees your first 5-star, Noelle. Use the starter wishes according to how the game goes; do not hurry.

  3. 3
    Collect chests and quests

    The chests and exploration quests you see on the map give wish materials and character growth resources in the first days.

  4. 4
    Do the daily commissions

    Daily Commissions give a small reward each day; spend only a short time on them.

  5. 5
    Develop your characters

    First level up your main character and one teammate you like; do not develop every new character in a scattered way.

  6. 6
    Check your bag

    Before selling materials in your bag, read what they are for; most will be needed later.

  7. 7
    Keep track of time

    The game saves automatically; but events are time-limited, so note the ones about to end.

Tip

The biggest mistake in the first days is trying to do everything at once. Finishing one region before moving to the next helps you understand the game and protects your resources.

Adventure Rank and game progression

#

Adventure Rank (AR) is your account's overall level. It rises as you explore, open chests and finish quests. To "ascend" your characters past level 40 a certain rank is required; if your rank is low, your character gets stuck at a certain level.

At rank 6 the domains that give gear up to level 90 (artifact domains) unlock; around rank 8-9 the world level ascension quests begin. As the world level rises, enemies get stronger but rewards improve too. If your rank is not enough, continue with quests and exploration; wishes do not raise your rank.

Tip

Raising the world level is not mandatory. If you struggle, stay at a lower world level and strengthen your characters and gear first.

How does the wish system work?

#

Wishes are the luck-based way to obtain characters and weapons. Some rules are written in the game and some are community measurements; keep the two apart.

TopicInformation
Character banner 5-star rateBase rate 0.6%; a 5-star is guaranteed within 90 wishes at the latest
Soft pityFrom about the 74th wish the chance rises (unofficial, community measurement)
50/50 ruleThe first 5-star is mostly 50% (about 55% with Capturing Radiance); if you lose, the next one is guaranteed
Weapon banner guarantee80 wishes
4-starGuaranteed at least once in 10 wishes
Pity carryoverCarries over within the same banner type; different banner types count separately

Watch out

Wishes can also be bought with money; but this is not required. Set your budget before spending and do not go past the limit with a "just one more" thought. Turn on parental controls to stop children from spending with card details.

Tip

Saving your wishes and using them on the character you want most gives a better result than spending at random. But no banner promises an outcome; the guarantee numbers are only upper limits.

Characters, teams and resource management

#
  • Team: using different elements together in a four-character team (for example water + fire, vaporize) gives a combined effect (elemental reaction).
  • Character role: one main damage dealer, one support or healer, and one character that links the elements is a good base for a team.
  • Weapon and gear: a character's weapon and artifacts are most of its strength; develop the weapon and the main artifact first.
  • Resin: used to claim rewards from domains and bosses and refills over time; try not to let it overflow.
  • Free characters: characters given through the story and events are also enough to build a good team; chasing every character is not required.

Simple rule: the game can be played free; if you have no money your progress only slows down, it does not stop.

Elemental reactions and team roles

#

The core idea of Genshin is combining elements. Applying different elements to the same target creates an elemental reaction. The table below is a short summary of the main reactions (consistent with the in-game tutorial and the official wiki): To trigger a reaction you first apply one element to the target and then hit it with a second element. Watch the element icon that appears on the enemy: if the icon is there, it is the right moment for a reaction. Applying the same element repeatedly gives no reaction, so you need to switch characters often.

Reactions that come with Dendro such as Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, Quicken, Aggravate and Spread are more advanced; Shatter also happens when you hit a frozen target with a heavy weapon. In the first days you only need to know Vaporize, Melt and Swirl. A practical rule for a beginner: one character applies the element and another hits the same target with a different element; there is no need to build long, complicated chains. Instead of memorizing reaction names, seeing each of them once in the short practice area of the in-game tutorial teaches faster.

Tip

In a four-character team two different elements are enough; three are not required. First build a reaction that works with two characters and get a feel for it with the tutorial and the map.

Resin budget and a daily routine

#

Original Resin is spent to get rewards from domains and bosses. Its cap is 200 and one regenerates every 8 minutes, so from empty it takes about 27 hours to fill. Fragile Resin is an extra resource worth 60 resin, and Condensed Resin is used to boost the reward from domains on a later run (see the in-game description for details). Resin is spent only in certain reward places (domains, bosses); things like exploration, chests and commissions do not ask for resin. So do not think the game is over when the resin runs out: when it is used up, you can return to exploring the map, the story or events. What matters is not leaving full resin idle for a long time.

  1. 1
    Do the daily commissions

    Finish the commission tasks every day; they take little time and give extra rewards.

  2. 2
    Spend your resin

    Without waiting for the resin to fill, enter the domain of the material you need most; do not let it pass the 200 cap.

  3. 3
    Do not skip weekly bosses

    Weekly bosses ask for discounted resin on the first runs (the number may change with versions).

  4. 4
    Check codes and events

    Event rewards are time-limited; put them on your calendar.

  5. 5
    Explore the map

    Chests and mysteries give resources without spending resin.

Tip

Make a resin budget: do not alternate an empty day and a full day. Regular small spending is more efficient than long sessions.

Redeeming codes and a free-to-play plan

#

Official codes (redeem codes) give in-game rewards. Redeem condition: usually Adventure Rank 10 is needed; some event codes may ask for different conditions. You can redeem codes on HoYoverse's official gift page (genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/gift) or in the game; rewards land in the in-game mailbox and are kept for about 30 days. The expiry of a currently valid code is 16 November 2026 at 00:00 (UTC+8). Whether codes are written in lower or upper case, they must be entered with the right characters; an expired or already used code gives an error. You cannot use the same code a second time on the same account. You receive the code rewards on first use, so collect the rewards right away without letting valid codes go to waste.

  1. 1
    Go to the official page

    Codes are entered only on the official code page or in the game.

  2. 2
    Enter it in the game

    Follow Paimon Menu > Settings > Account > Redeem Code.

  3. 3
    Check the mail

    The reward arrives in the mail; collect it before it expires.

Take codes only from official sources. Free-code sites that ask for a password, a verification code or a login are scams.

#

Reaching a high rank is not worth rushing; as your rank rises, enemies get stronger. If your characters are not ready, staying at a low world level is fine. Instead of speeding up the rank, doing story quests and exploration at their natural pace keeps both character growth and resource gathering in balance. If you get stuck in an area, checking character level, weapon and artifacts is often more effective than raising your rank; so strengthen your roster first.

Common beginner mistakes

#
  • Spending the starter wishes without thinking.
  • Trying to develop every new character at once and spreading resources thin.
  • Letting resin overflow or never using it.
  • Entering a hard area without developing weapons and artifacts, then blaming the character.
  • Missing event deadlines; writing free-reward events into a calendar is enough.

Watch out

Treat wishes as a game of chance. Always keep the option of putting the same budget toward another character in a later version.

Account security and a spending warning

#

Your account carries hours of your effort. Do not share your password, verification code or email access with anyone.

  • Sites and apps promising free primogems, free wishes or a "generator" are scams; they take over your account or steal your card details. The game's real free sources are only in-game quests, events and codes announced through official channels.
  • Redeem codes usually come from livestreams and official channels; type a code only into the game's own code screen or the official site, and never enter your password anywhere else.
  • Buying or selling accounts, and using unauthorized software or bots, break the rules and get the account banned.
  • Turn on two-step verification; give your HoYoverse account a separate, strong password.
  • On children's accounts use a spending limit and parental controls.

Watch out

No one asks for your game password "to give you wishes". If a suspicious offer arrives, refuse it and change your password right away.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is Genshin Impact free?

Yes, downloading and playing is free. There are optional purchases; but the main content can be played without buying.

Is the game in Turkish?

Yes, Turkish text is supported (16 text languages according to the Epic store page); the voice-over is Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean.

Which devices does it run on?

PC, PlayStation, iOS and Android; for other platforms check the official site.

How do I get my first 5-star character?

The beginner banner guarantees Noelle. Other 5-star characters need the wish system and time.

Can I trust free primogem sites?

No. All of them are scams; their aim is to steal your account.

Should I get every new character?

No. You can play all the content with the free ones and one or two you like.
