What EA SPORTS FC 27 is and where it stands in 2026

#

EA SPORTS FC is an annual football simulation series. The current edition is EA SPORTS FC 27, released on 25 September 2026. Buyers of the Ultimate and Ultimate Plus editions got up to seven days of early access. It includes Ultimate Team, where you build your own squad, Career, where you run a club or develop one player, the new social mode The Grounds, and classic matches.

The main game is paid, but EA also offers a free, limited FC 27 Lite version. It includes Kick-Off, Learn to Play, Online Friendlies and Online Seasons; it does not include Ultimate Team, Manager Career or The Grounds, and it needs a constant internet connection. It is a good way to try the game before buying.

Tip

This guide explains principles; player ratings, SBCs and events change weekly. For the current state, check EA's official announcement pages.

Before you start: platform, edition and requirements

#

FC 27 officially launched on the EA app, Steam, Epic Games Store, PS4, PS5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2. The Grounds (Clubs included) is only on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Switch 2. Turkish is also in the language list on EA's buy page.

  • PC minimum: 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, Ryzen 5 1600 or Core i5 6600K, 8 GB dual-channel DDR4 RAM, Radeon RX 570 or GeForce GTX 1050 Ti, DirectX 12, 100 GB of free space.
  • PC recommended: Ryzen 7 2700X or Core i7 6700, 12 GB RAM, Radeon RX 5600 XT or GeForce GTX 1660, 100 GB of SSD space.
  • Account: Ultimate Team and online modes need an EA account and an internet connection.
  • Cross-play: per EA Help it exists between PC, PlayStation and Xbox and is on by default on first launch; players are grouped by generation: PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC together, PS4 and Xbox One separately. Switch and Switch 2 have no cross-play.
  • Cross-progression: per EA Help it applies only to Ultimate Team and within the same platform family: it carries from PS4 to PS5 or Xbox One to Xbox Series X|S, not from PlayStation to Xbox.
  • Price: EA's store shows the price by region; an official Turkish lira price is not disclosed. See the current price on the page of your own store.

Tip

Try the free FC 27 Lite first; if you like the basic match feel, buy the full game. If you want Career or Ultimate Team, Lite is not enough.

Your first steps: getting into Ultimate Team

#

Ultimate Team starts free: you build a squad from starter players and earn packs, coins and players as you play. EA's beginners page recommends this order.

  1. 1
    Pick your starter pack

    Do not overthink this choice; if you have a favorite league, pick it. The starter pack is only the first step.

  2. 2
    Complete the Foundations objectives

    The series runs: The Journey Begins, First Team Dreams, Rising Through the Ranks, Market Madness, Chasing Greatness and Total Football. It teaches the game while giving players, packs and FUT Coins.

  3. 3
    Play against the AI

    Start with modes against the AI such as Moments and Squad Battles. EA suggests playing Squad Battles on Amateur or Semi-Pro difficulty with your starting squad in the early days.

  4. 4
    Unlock the Transfer Market

    For new players, the objective groups The Journey Begins, First Team Dreams and Rising Through the Ranks are completed in order; the last one unlocks Transfer Market and Champions access.

  5. 5
    Claim rewards after every match

    Do not leave earned packs, coins and season pass XP unclaimed; small gains grow the squad over time.

Tip

Gaining a little each day is enough. EA itself describes early progress as needing more engagement, becoming easier through steady play and skill.

When you start Career, begin with a small goal: bring through youth academy players or build a balanced squad for a single season. Do not try to manage everything at once.

Defending and attacking basics

#

The meta changes, but basic football logic does not. The principles below work in every edition.

  • Defending - distance: do not lunge at the ball. Stay close to the opponent, slow him down and close the passing lane; every tackle attempt can leave you out of position.
  • Defending - player choice: select the player nearest the ball by hand; send back a player who breaks the defensive line. Do not send two players to the same ball.
  • Attacking - patience: do not shoot at every chance. Open space, pass sideways and shoot from a prepared position.
  • Attacking - movement: players without the ball build the play too. Runs from midfield behind the defence push it back and open passing angles.
  • Shooting: tap and release to set power; an over-powered shot rarely finds the goal. Placing it from close range scores more often than a hard shot from distance.
  • Transition after losing the ball: in the first three seconds, either press or drop back; being caught between the two concedes the most goals.

Tip

In one match try to improve only one thing: this match only defending distance, the next only shot power. Focusing on five things at once settles none of them.

Settings and controller setup

#

Menu names can change between versions; the suggestions below are at principle level.

  • Difficulty: start at Amateur or Semi-Pro; EA recommends this for newcomers. When matches begin to feel comfortable, go up one level.
  • Assists: leave pass and shot assistance on at first. As you get used to it, reduce assistance for more control.
  • Camera: choose a camera that lets you see the game comfortably and keep it for at least ten matches; constant changes disrupt adaptation.
  • Controls: try the control scheme in Learn to Play or Moments. Moments teaches the basic controls.
  • Assists in Rush: per EA small-sided modes default to manual shooting and fully assisted passing. In a full match you choose those two yourself; manual shooting asks more skill.
  • Controller habits: do not hold sprint all the time; use it in short bursts when running with the ball. In defence keep press and jockey where your fingers reach easily (button names vary by platform, see the in-game Controls screen).
  • Stick drift: if a player drifts by himself while the controller is idle, the stick is usually the cause; first check your platform's controller settings and clean the controller.
  • Audio and subtitles: Turkish is listed on the buy page; for commentary language, look in the in-game language settings.

Tip

Before changing settings, test them in Learn to Play or a friendly. Fiddling with settings during an online ranked match is frustrating.

Free-start plan: the first two weeks

#

The plan below is based on the order in EA's beginners page and the Transfer Market rule; the split into days is a suggestion. It aims to progress without spending any FC Points.

  1. 1
    Day 1: Foundations

    Start with The Journey Begins and finish the next objectives. You learn the basic controls in Moments; rewards come as players, packs and FUT Coins.

  2. 2
    Days 2-3: the Transfer Market gate

    Finish First Team Dreams and then Rising Through the Ranks. According to EA the last one unlocks Transfer Market and Champions; full access arrives within 48 hours on PC and 24 hours on other platforms. While waiting you can list players but cannot search or Bid/Buy Now.

  3. 3
    Days 3-5: Squad Battles

    Play against AI squads on Amateur or Semi-Pro difficulty. EA says each match in FC 27 gives more FUT Coins and Season Points, and Squad Battles now also awards Champions Qualification Points.

  4. 4
    Days 5-7: your first SBCs

    Player and Upgrade SBCs are streamlined: eligible cards from your club are listed and you pick which to submit; an item's value comes from its OVR, rarity and Holographic status. Hand in only spare cards, not ones you use.

  5. 5
    Days 8-10: first steps in Rivals

    Once your squad has settled, enter Division Rivals. EA says Rivals rewards differ more clearly between divisions, Bounties return more often, and all packs earned in Rivals are tradeable at launch.

  6. 6
    Days 11-14: the road to Champions

    To enter Champions you collect Champions Qualification Points (CQP); they come from both Rivals and Squad Battles. The Division 6 requirement is gone in FC 27. Finals are 15 matches; on your first try the goal is learning your squad and rhythm, not the ranking.

Tip

Repeat the same order each week: Objectives, Squad Battles, then Rivals. One hour a day is enough for weekly rewards; small and steady beats a burst of heavy play followed by quitting.

Tip

Not officially disclosed: the exact division thresholds in Rivals and the CQP amount needed for Champions; check your own balance in the in-game Champions tab.

Ultimate Team: Rivals, Champions and rewards

#
  • Campaign Hub: reward paths such as Objectives, SBCs, Evolutions and live events are gathered in one place; EA describes this as the Campaign Hub.
  • Division Rivals: per EA the reward gap between divisions grows in FC 27; for example Division 1 upgraded rewards include 95,000 FUT Coins, 84+ and 83+ Gold packs, a Giant Gold Pack and a TOTW pack. Bounties return more often.
  • Champions: the 15-match Finals structure continues. Champions Qualification Points are earned in both Rivals and Squad Battles; the Division 6 requirement is gone. A higher division earns CQP faster, but your division no longer decides whether you can qualify.
  • Web and Companion apps: Transfer Market access there follows a separate rule; further detail is not officially disclosed, so check your own account's status in the app.
  • Packs: they contain random players and cosmetic items. Packs you earn as rewards cost nothing to open; packs you buy with real money involve chance.

Tip

When you gain Transfer Market access, do not dive into trading right away. Watch prices for a few days, see which players' prices move, then try small amounts.

The chemistry system: how players link up

#

Chemistry in Ultimate Team shows how well the players in your squad fit together through club, league and nation links. Chemistry rises as players with a shared club, league or nation sit next to each other, and a player gets full benefit in their own position (exact numbers can change by version, so check the in-game chemistry screen).

  • Official change in FC 27: ICONs get full chemistry when played in position and give a +1 league link to every league; their nation contribution drops from +2 to +1.
  • Heroes: they still get full chemistry in position; their league contribution drops from +2 to +1 and their nation contribution stays +1. Hall of FUT items follow the same rules as Heroes.
  • Special cards: EA says it will be more selective about boosted chemistry for campaign and other special items through the season; do not assume every new special card fixes your chemistry.
  • Practical rule: pick a league or nation core first, then fill the gaps. A mid-rated squad with high chemistry often plays more steadily than a high-rated squad with scattered chemistry.

Tip

When changing your squad after an SBC or a pack, look at the chemistry screen first. Putting a player out of position can lower chemistry and wipe out the rating advantage.

Transfer Market basics

#

The Transfer Market in Ultimate Team is a player-to-player market where cards are bought and sold for FUT Coins. According to EA all pack rewards in Rivals are tradeable at launch and a larger share of rewards comes directly as FUT Coins and tradeable packs, so you can put what you earn to work on the market.

  • Access: on a new account it opens once the three Foundations objective groups are done; full access arrives within 48 hours on PC and 24 hours on other platforms (EA Help).
  • Patience: the best buyer is not the one in a hurry but the one who waits. Before buying a card that looks unusually cheap, check other listings of the same card.
  • Sale fee: a fee is taken from sales on the market; check the rate on the in-game screen and plan with it.
  • Price tracking: watch the target card's price for a few days. When a new event or SBC arrives, the market can move fast.
  • Goal: strengthening the squad, not profit. Collect your coins toward a target card; random buying and selling eats time and coins.

Watch out

Use only the game's own market. Buying or selling coins through third parties, automated trading bots and account sales can get your account closed; stay away from them.

Common beginner mistakes

#
  • Playing humans straight away. EA recommends playing against the AI first; entering online matches before the squad and controls are settled leads to disappointment.
  • Skipping Foundations. These objectives teach and reward; skip them and Transfer Market access is delayed too.
  • Spending everything from your first packs. Tie coins and players to a goal; random spending scatters the squad.
  • Looking only at ratings. Position and chemistry can make a lower-rated but well-placed player more valuable.
  • Tilting after a loss and messing with settings. Take a short break after a loss and review one match to find the cause.

Tip

Think through a lost match with a calm head: how did the goal go in, where did the defence open up? A single answer helps in the next match.

Account security and spending warning

#

EA's buy page states that the game has optional in-game purchases of virtual currency for a random selection of items, and the rating body ESRB lists "includes random items". What you get from packs bought with FC Points is not known in advance, so set your budget up front.

  • Set a monthly spending limit and stop when you reach it. Spending more to win back a loss is the most common mistake.
  • Add two-step verification to your EA account, do not reuse your password elsewhere and do not leave a payment method saved.
  • For children, turn on your platform's parental controls and require approval for FC Points spending.
  • Links and sites promising free FC Points or coins are scams or account-theft attempts; enter your password only on the official EA page.

Watch out

Buying or selling coins through third parties, buying or selling accounts, automated trading bots, cheat software, having someone boost your rank, and betting on game outcomes are prohibited and risky. Your account can be permanently closed; stay away from all of it.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is FC 27 free?

The main game is paid. There is a free, limited FC 27 Lite; it does not include Ultimate Team or Manager Career.

Is Turkish available?

Turkish is in the language list on EA's buy page. For commentary language, check the in-game settings.

Do I have to spend money on Ultimate Team?

No. You earn packs, coins and players by playing matches and completing objectives; spending money is optional.

How do I get into the Transfer Market?

On a new account, finish The Journey Begins, First Team Dreams and Rising Through the Ranks in order. Full access opens within 48 hours on PC and 24 hours on other platforms.

Are there cross-play and cross-progression?

Cross-play exists between PC, PlayStation and Xbox, but players are grouped by generation and Switch is outside it. Cross-progression is only in Ultimate Team and within the same platform family.

Which platforms have The Grounds?

PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Switch 2. It is not on PS4, Xbox One or the original Switch.

What changed in Career mode?

There is a rebuilt Transfer Market, Dynamic OVR, new clauses in negotiation and Manager Live Creator Challenges, among other changes.
