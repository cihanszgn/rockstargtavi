What Mobile Legends is and where it stands in 2026

#

In Mobile Legends: Bang Bang two teams of five face off. Each player picks a hero and the goal is to destroy the crystal in the enemy base. The game is designed for touchscreens; matches are short and comfortable to play on a phone.

According to the Google Play page, Season 42 started on September 16, 2026 at 00:00 server time. The seasonal skin is 'Flamehide Thane' for Popol and Kupa and the border is 'Starward Decade'; there are also 10th Anniversary revamps for Masha and Bruno. The game presents itself as 'Play to Win, not Pay to Win'.

The latest patch number is not officially stated; community sources give 2.2.16a (14 October 2026). Season 43 is expected to start in mid-December; the date is not certain. The 2026 Annual Map Change went live on 28 September 2026 (according to community sources), with additions such as the Healing Turtle, Golden Turret and Revealing Wisps.

Tip

Numbers in this guide are accurate as of November 2026. Some information comes from community sources and is marked in the text; verify it in the game.

Before you start: install, account, system

#

In a new account, first finish the tutorial and the first tasks; they teach the basic controls and also give starter rewards. Before spending what you earn on your first heroes, try to work out which role you like.

You download the game from Google Play or the App Store. According to the Google Play page the game is rated 12+ and contains in-app purchases. You use an email or a linked account for your account.

  • To play on PC you use Google Play Games. Requirements: Windows 10 version 2004, 10 GB of free SSD space, Intel UHD 630, 4 physical cores, 8 GB of RAM and virtualization turned on.
  • Keep your network stable; use Wi-Fi or good mobile data.
  • The server time may differ from your local time; check season start and end times in the game.

Watch out

Download the game only from the official store. Sites and apps promising 'free diamonds' or accounts can get your account stolen.

The most important thing for your phone is a solid connection. Use Wi-Fi or reliable mobile data; a dropped connection during a match punishes both you and your team. Battery and heat matter too: in long sessions, plugging the phone in and removing the case reduces heating. With headphones you hear footsteps and ability sounds better.

Your first matches step by step

#
  1. 1
    Finish the tutorial

    Learn the movement, attack and ability buttons.

  2. 2
    Play against bots

    Try heroes and the map in bot matches until you feel comfortable.

  3. 3
    Move on to Classic matches

    Get used to it by playing unranked against real players.

  4. 4
    Prepare for Ranked

    According to community sources Ranked needs account level 8 and enough heroes; Moonton has not published this on its own page, so the in-game lock screen is what counts.

  5. 5
    Keep play sessions short

    Taking a break after one or two matches reduces mistakes.

In the first days your aim is not winning but learning the map and your hero. In a match watch only two things: which lane you are in and when the next objective (tower, Turtle, Lord) arrives. After the match look at the statistics on screen, find the mistake you made most, and in the next match try to fix only that. This method improves you faster than playing a lot at random.

Choosing a hero and role for the first weeks

#

The game has five basic roles: Tank, Mage, Marksman, Assassin and Support. There are also Fighter heroes. Heroes are unlocked through the free rotation or the store. The examples below are common beginner picks that have stayed simple for a long time, not an official Moonton recommendation; balance may change with patches.

Tip

For a week, play with the same two heroes. Learning one well moves you forward faster than trying many.

If you do not know which to choose, start with a Tank in the Roam position: standing in front of your teammates without dying brings no mechanical difficulty and teaches you the map. A second option is the Marksman; you farm safely in the Gold Lane and watch the map.

Emblem and battle spell choice

#

Before every match you make two choices: an emblem set and a battle spell. Both depend on the hero's role, and for the first weeks the game's recommended ready choice is the safest.

ChoiceWhat it doesWho it suits
EmblemEach type (Tank, Assassin, Mage, Marksman, Support, Fighter) gives fixed power by role and lets you pick three talentsUse the emblem recommended in the hero's description
RetributionDeals big damage to jungle monsters, Turtle and LordJungler only; you cannot jungle with another spell
FlickerShort-range teleportMost heroes that want to escape or start a fight

The number and effects of spells and emblems can change with seasons; follow the current description you see in the shop or the hero page. After picking a spell, play a week with the same choice: memorizing its cooldown makes every match easier.

Tip

If you are the jungler, save Retribution for Turtle and Lord; do not waste it on small camps.

Core mechanics: lanes, jungle, objectives

#

The map consists of three main paths (lanes) with a jungle between them. The lanes: Gold Lane (usually a marksman), EXP Lane (usually a fighter), Mid Lane (usually a mage), Jungle and Roam (roaming; usually a tank or support). The first Turtle appears on the EXP lane side.

  • Turrets: Outer turrets fall first, then inner ones, and finally the crystal in the main base.
  • Jungle monsters: They give gold and levels; objectives like the Turtle and the Lord add power to the team.
  • Minions: They are killed for gold; you earn gold with the last hit.
  • Map awareness: Look at the minimap often; an enemy leaving their lane is a danger sign.
TermMeaning
FarmCollecting gold by killing minions and monsters
GankSurprising another lane to kill the opponent
RotateThe team moving between lanes
PushMoving the minion wave toward the enemy turret
RetreatPulling back in a moment of danger

A new player's most lasting gain is looking at the minimap and moving with the team. Chasing alone often ends in death.

Turtle and Lord timings

#

Turtle and Lord are the main neutral objectives that give the team gold, experience and power. According to community sources the first Turtle spawns around 2:00 and respawns about every 2 minutes; after the 2026 map change the Turtle's behavior (for example the Healing Turtle) is reported to have been updated. The exact spawn minute of the Lord is not consistent between sources and Moonton has not officially stated it; so watch the in-game timer.

ObjectiveWhenWhat it gives
TurtleFirst spawn around 2:00; respawns in about 2 minutesGold, experience and a shield for the team; the hero landing the last hit gets an instant heal and an extra shield
LordSpawns in the mid game; exact minute not officially statedA strong helper that pushes side lanes; speeds up tower breaking
  1. 1
    Prepare 20-25 seconds before the spawn

    Set up vision and check the entry bushes; whoever arrives late loses the first fight.

  2. 2
    Leave Retribution to the jungler

    Do not all hit the objective at once; hold damage until the Retribution range.

  3. 3
    Push the wave first

    Bring Mid and Roam to the objective together.

  4. 4
    Do not force it if unsafe

    If the enemy controls the bush, take another objective or hit a tower.

Tip

Before fighting for an objective, count where the enemy jungler is and whether your teammates are alive.

Settings: starting values

#

The most useful setting is the button layout. If your thumb struggles to reach an ability, move that button; being able to make the same move every match is worth more than fine graphics tuning.

These are not official values; they are commonly suggested starting points. Try them in a bot match.

When you change a setting, play a few matches with the same value; do not change your mind right away.

Diamonds and spending budget

#

Mobile Legends is free to play; the Google Play page presents the game as 'Play to Win, not Pay to Win' and notes in-app purchases. Diamonds are real money; prices differ by region, so see the current amount in the in-game store. Battle Points (BP) are earned by playing and used to unlock heroes.

PriorityWhat to doWhy
1. HeroesUnlock heroes with BP; do not spend diamondsWhat shapes your power is the hero, not a skin
2. Monthly pass (Starlight etc.)Consider it only if you will really playCosmetics and small rewards; exact content and price are in the store
3. SkinsLeave them for lastThey are cosmetic; they give no game power
  • Set a monthly cap and talk about spending with an adult depending on your age.
  • Stay away from third-party 'cheap diamond' sites and top-up services that ask for your account password.
  • If you regret a purchase, set a rule of buying nothing for two weeks.

Watch out

Sites promising 'cheap diamonds' or 'free diamonds' can get your account stolen; use only the official in-game store.

Common beginner mistakes

#

Most mistakes come from habit rather than missing knowledge. After every match note just one thing: why did I die, which objective did I miss. If the same answer comes up in three or four matches, that is what you actually need to work on.

  • Jumping into ranked unprepared; get used to it with Classic matches first.
  • Diving a turret alone without waiting for the team.
  • Not looking at the minimap.
  • Blaming teammates; when you die, look for the mistake in yourself.
  • Trying a different hero every match.
  • Playing back-to-back matches while angry; take a break.

A common mistake is joining every fight. Mobile Legends is played in short matches and one death costs towers and objectives because of the respawn time. If your health is low, pull back and heal; when you see an enemy vanish from the map, watch the bushes. The second mistake is never looking at the minimap: a glance every second or two lets you notice an enemy coming to a side lane in advance.

The third mistake is focusing only on kill count. What wins matches is breaking towers and taking objectives such as the Turtle and Lord; a kill is only valuable when it opens a chance for those. Attacking teammates in chat also loses games: a short, polite ping is far more effective than an angry message. If you feel tilted, take a break instead of starting the next match.

Account safety and behavior rules

#

For younger players: check the in-app purchase and chat settings together with an adult. Do not click links from people you do not know and do not share personal information (address, school, phone) in chat.

Your account is your digital vault. Link it to a secure email and do not share your password with other sites.

Watch out

Cheat software, third-party mod apps, scripts, rank boosting services, buying or selling accounts, skin gambling or betting sites and pirate or fake 'free diamond' sites can lead to a permanent ban. Stay away from them.

  • Constantly going AFK or deliberately ruining a match brings penalties and lowers your Credit Score.
  • Be respectful to teammates; report and mute toxic chat.
  • Playing on a second account against lower-ranked players (smurfing) is unfair; do not do it.
  • Never share your account with anyone.

To protect your account use a strong password used only for this game, turn on two-step verification if possible, and share your account details with nobody. Even official support does not ask for your password. Rank boosting, buying or selling accounts and banned programs both break the rules and can get your account closed; stay away from all of them. Your own progress is worth far more than someone else's, and it keeps the game fun.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is Mobile Legends free?

Yes. The game is free; purchases are for cosmetics.

Can I play on PC?

Yes, through Google Play Games; the requirements are Windows 10 version 2004, 10 GB of SSD space and virtualization on.

When can I play ranked?

According to community sources at account level 8 with enough heroes; Moonton has not officially published it, so the in-game lock is what counts.

Can my rank drop?

You lose stars; losses in Warrior cost no stars (according to community sources).

Which hero should I start with?

Pick a simple, durable hero; play the same two for a week.

Is there Turkish language?

Not officially stated; check the in-game language settings.
