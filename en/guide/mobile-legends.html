What Mobile Legends is and where it stands in 2026

#

In Mobile Legends: Bang Bang two teams of five face off. Each player picks a hero and the goal is to destroy the crystal in the enemy base. The game is designed for touchscreens; matches are short and comfortable to play on a phone.

According to the Google Play page, Season 42 started on September 16, 2026 at 00:00 server time. The seasonal skin is 'Flamehide Thane' for Popol and Kupa and the border is 'Starward Decade'; there are also 10th Anniversary revamps for Masha and Bruno. The game presents itself as 'Play to Win, not Pay to Win'.

The latest patch appears in a secondary source as 2.2.16a (October 14, 2026); a new update in mid-November was reported but its number was not verified. Season 43 is expected to start in mid-December, which is not confirmed.

Tip

Numbers in this guide are accurate as of November 2026. Some information is from secondary sources and is marked; verify it in the game.

Before you start: install, account, system

#

In a new account, first finish the tutorial and the first tasks; they teach the basic controls and also give starter rewards. Before spending what you earn on your first heroes, try to work out which role you like.

You download the game from Google Play or the App Store. According to the Google Play page the game is rated 12+ and contains in-app purchases. You use an email or a linked account for your account.

  • To play on PC you use Google Play Games. Requirements: Windows 10 version 2004, 10 GB of free SSD space, Intel UHD 630, 4 physical cores, 8 GB of RAM and virtualization turned on.
  • Keep your network stable; use Wi-Fi or good mobile data.
  • The server time may differ from your local time; check season start and end times in the game.

Watch out

Download the game only from the official store. Sites and apps promising 'free diamonds' or accounts can get your account stolen.

#
  1. 1

    Learn the movement, attack and ability buttons.

  2. 2
    Play against bots

    Try heroes and the map in bot matches until you feel comfortable.

  3. 3
    Move on to Classic matches

    Get used to it by playing unranked against real players.

  4. 4

  5. 5

    Taking a break after one or two matches reduces mistakes.

Choosing a hero and role for the first weeks

#

The game has five core roles: Tank, Mage, Marksman, Assassin and Support. There are also Fighter-type heroes. Heroes unlock through the free rotation or the shop. The examples below show choices that have stayed simple for years (my own knowledge; balance can change).

Tip

For a week, play with the same two heroes. Learning one well moves you forward faster than trying many.

Core mechanics: lanes, jungle, objectives

#

The map consists of three main paths (lanes) with a jungle between them. The lanes: Gold Lane (usually a marksman), EXP Lane (usually a fighter), Mid Lane (usually a mage), Jungle and Roam (roaming; usually a tank or support). The first Turtle appears on the EXP lane side.

  • Turrets: Outer turrets fall first, then inner ones, and finally the crystal in the main base.
  • Jungle monsters: They give gold and levels; objectives like the Turtle and the Lord add power to the team.
  • Minions: They are killed for gold; you earn gold with the last hit.
  • Map awareness: Look at the minimap often; an enemy leaving their lane is a danger sign.

A new player's most lasting gain is looking at the minimap and moving with the team. Chasing alone often ends in death.

Settings: starting values

#

The most useful setting is the button layout. If your thumb struggles to reach an ability, move that button; being able to make the same move in every match is worth more than fine graphics tuning. I suggest this from general experience (my own knowledge).

These are not official values; they are commonly suggested starting points. Try them in a bot match.

SettingStarting suggestion
Button layoutEnlarge or move the buttons a little so your thumbs are comfortable
Auto targetLeave automatic target selection on for attacks
GraphicsMatch your phone; smoothness matters more than looks
AudioWear headphones; ability sounds carry information
NetworkWi-Fi or good mobile data; watch the ping on the in-game indicator

Tip

When you change a setting, play a few matches with the same value; do not change your mind right away.

Ranked system and rank table

#

The rank table can change in detail from season to season; star counts and tier names may be adjusted by updates. So do not treat the values below as final; compare them with the ranking page on your own screen.

According to secondary sources the ranked tiers are: Warrior, Elite, Master, Grandmaster, Epic, Legend and Mythic. Mythic has extra titles: Mythical Honor, Mythical Glory and Mythical Immortal. After Mythic the star count keeps running.

Common beginner mistakes

#

Most mistakes come from habit rather than missing knowledge. After every match note just one thing: why did I die, which objective did I miss. If the same answer comes up in three or four matches, that is what you actually need to work on.

  • Jumping into ranked unprepared; get used to it with Classic matches first.
  • Diving a turret alone without waiting for the team.
  • Not looking at the minimap.
  • Blaming teammates; when you die, look for the mistake in yourself.
  • Trying a different hero every match.
  • Playing back-to-back matches while angry; take a break.

Account safety and behavior rules

#

For younger players: check the in-app purchase and chat settings together with an adult. Do not click links from people you do not know and do not share personal information (address, school, phone) in chat.

Your account is your digital vault. Link it to a secure email and do not share your password with other sites.

Watch out

Cheat software, third-party mod apps, scripts, rank boosting services, buying or selling accounts, skin gambling or betting sites and pirate or fake 'free diamond' sites can lead to a permanent ban. Stay away from them.

  • Constantly going AFK or deliberately ruining a match brings penalties and lowers your Credit Score.
  • Be respectful to teammates; report and mute toxic chat.
  • Playing on a second account against lower-ranked players (smurfing) is unfair; do not do it.
  • Never share your account with anyone.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is Mobile Legends free?

Yes. The game is free; purchases are for cosmetics.

Can I play on PC?

Yes, through Google Play Games; the requirements are Windows 10 version 2004, 10 GB of SSD space and virtualization on.

When can I play ranked?

When the lock the game shows opens; sources list different conditions, so we give no exact number.

Can my rank drop?

You lose stars; losses in Warrior cost no stars (secondary source).

Which hero should I start with?

Pick a simple, durable hero; play the same two for a week.

Is there Turkish language?

Not verified in this guide; check the in-game language settings.
