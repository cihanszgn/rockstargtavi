Where the game stands in 2026

#

Knight Online is a long-running online role-playing game published in Turkey by NTTGame. The core of the game is the war between two nations: El Morad and Karus. The nations have no difference in strength; the choice comes down to looks and where your friends are.

This year has an important date: NTTGame opened the new servers it announced on 1 October 2026 on Thursday 12 November 2026 at 16:00 Turkey time. According to news reports the new servers are named Atlas, Oracle, Luna and Carnac. The new servers have no Ardream or Ronark Land Base maps; the fighting happens in Ronark Land. It was also announced that some servers will be merged later.

The existing USKO servers are listed as Zero, Pandora, Agartha, Felis, Minark, Dryads, Oreads and Destan. SteamKO, played through Steam, is a separate client with its own server (Zion).

The difference between old and new servers is mostly crowding and the economy. On a new server everyone starts from zero; item prices and competition are more balanced. Old servers have years of accumulated items and strong clans. Decide which suits you by your playstyle.

Tip

The server list and merger plans change. Before choosing a server, check NTTGame's current announcement.

Before you start: account, client, security

#

The game is free. You open an NTTGame account on the official site and take the client from the same site's download page. The Steam version (SteamKO) is separate; Steam asks for phone verification on the first login and it has in-app purchases. Decide up front which one you will play, because accounts and servers are separate on each side.

The new servers opened through pre-registration: 15-29 October was the first window, 29 October-12 November the second (server choice), and 10-11 November was character creation. Accounts that did not pre-register could not enter the new servers. Phone verification and OTP+ (a one-time-password app) were mandatory; OTP+ replaced AnyOTP on 15 October. Check NTTGame's announcement for whether you can enter a new server right now.

Watch out

The official system requirements page lists very old Windows versions. The real need today is very low; do not give up on the game because of the old values on that page.

The first hours, step by step

#
  1. 1
    Open the account

    Create an account on the NTTGame site, verify your e-mail and phone, and link the OTP+ app.

  2. 2
    Download the client

    Install the client from the official download page. Do not run an installer you got from a third-party site.

  3. 3
    Choose a server and character

    Pick the same server as your friends; changing servers is not easy. Choose your nation (El Morad or Karus) and class.

  4. 4
    Meet the town

    The game's hub is Moradon. Visit the quest NPCs (non-player characters) and learn skills from the window you open with the K key.

  5. 5
    Get a pet

    At level 10 you get a pet from Familiar Trainer Kate in Moradon.

  6. 6
    Form a party

    The level gap within a party can be at most 8. Progress in the same level range as your friends.

Playing alone at the start is possible, but forming a party speeds up leveling noticeably.

In the first hours the goal is not fast leveling but learning the interface and commands. Opening the chat, party and merchant windows once and trying them shortens the later hours. When you get stuck you can ask in the clan or server chat, but do not answer anyone who asks you for money or a password.

Tip

If you do not know the stat points when creating a character, do not assign them at random; read the class's recommended spread first. You gain 3 points per level.

Choosing a nation and class

#

There are two nations: El Morad and Karus. The nations are equal in strength; class options differ a little by nation. Four shared classes (Warrior, Rogue, Mage, Priest) exist on both sides; Portu is specific to El Morad and Kurian to Karus.

ClassRoleWho it suits
WarriorMelee, sturdyPlayers who want a simple, solid start
RogueAgile, fast damagePlayers who like speed and movement
MageSpell damagePlayers who want to hit from range
PriestHealing and supportPlayers who want to keep the team alive

The stats are STR, DEX, HP, INT and MP; spread them to suit your class. Resetting stats can be costly, so the first spread matters.

Tip

Warrior is the simplest first character. Choosing a class the team needs also makes finding a party easier.

Leveling: a fast and efficient route

#

Leveling is basically killing monsters in the zone, doing quests and forming parties. The level cap is 83; a player at the cap who collects 100% EXP at level 83 can do a rebirth. A rebirth needs three quests from Fortune Reader Mackin, 1 Gold Bar and 10,000 contribution points; each rebirth level gives 2 extra stat points. Conditions can change with updates, so confirm on the in-game NPC screen.

  • Be sure to finish the quest NPCs in the first 20 levels; they give experience and gear.
  • If your level is not enough for a zone, do not hesitate to go back to a lower-level monster zone.
  • Achievements open with the /Achieve command and give regular rewards.

If the level gap within a party exceeds 8 you cannot play together. So make an effort to level at the same pace as your friends.

As you approach rebirth, do not rush: preparing the required quests, the 1 Gold Bar and the 10,000 contribution points in advance saves you time once you reach level 83. Instead of doing the preparation in the first days after the server opens, complete it little by little while leveling; verify the current conditions again in the game and in official announcements.

Tip

You lose EXP when you die. Do not risk expensive magic gear and a zone beyond your strength at the same time.

Money and economy: in-game routes only

#

The in-game currency is Noah. Income comes from monster hunting, quest rewards and item trade. With the merchant command you can open your own shop and sell items. Selling to NPCs and trading between players is also possible. The warehouse is tied to the account within a server.

Item upgrading uses the Magic Anvil next to Charon in Moradon. An upgrade can fail and the item can be destroyed; so do not use valuable items for experiments and start only with items you can afford to lose. Success rates are written in the game and in official announcements.

Follow a simple order for income: quest rewards first, then drops from hunting, and the merchant last. Do not sell at the merchant without checking an item's average price; the same item can sell at different prices from server to server. Prices keep changing, so never treat any figure here as fixed.

Watch out

Selling in-game currency, items or accounts to other players for real money (RMT) and using third-party sites for it breaks the game rules and gets your account closed. Do not trade outside the official PUS.

After level 83: war and progression

#

At 83 the game is shaped around war and equipment. On the new servers all PvP (player versus player) happens in Ronark Land, since the Ardream and Ronark Land Base maps are absent. Clans and nation wars are at the center of the late game.

  • Gain stat points through rebirth.
  • Follow achievements regularly.
  • Join a clan and take part in events together.

Premium products (for example Switching Premium and War Premium) give some advantages and are bought with NPoints. Read what a product gives on the official page before buying.

Tip

In the first weeks new servers are flooded with players; leveling in a crowd can slow down. Avoid the queue by changing zones.

Common mistakes and safety

#
  • Downloading the client from unofficial sites.
  • Using the same password on other sites.
  • Playing without OTP+ and phone verification.

Watch out

Stay away from cheats, bots, macros, exploits, private servers, account and in-game currency selling (RMT) and pirated clients. They lead to account closure and theft of your data. Private servers are unofficial and put your account at risk.

Keep your registered e-mail address and phone up to date; you need them when you have to recover the account. If someone you do not know says 'I will double your items' or 'share the code' in the game, it is a scam.

NTTGame will not ask for your password or OTP code. Log in only on official sites and always check the address.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is Knight Online free?

Yes, it is free to play. Premium and cosmetic products are optional.

Can I still enter the new servers?

The launch went through pre-registration. Check NTTGame's announcement for the entry conditions right now.

Which nation should I choose?

Both are equal in strength. Pick the one your friends are in.

What is the level cap?

83, followed by the rebirth system. Confirm the conditions in the game.

Can I play on a phone?

No. The game runs only on Windows.

Is the Steam version the same as the official client?

No, SteamKO is a separate client with a separate server. Accounts are not shared.
