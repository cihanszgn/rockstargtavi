Where the game stands in 2026

#

Metin2 is an old and beloved Korean online RPG developed by Webzen and published by Gameforge; it is free to play and, according to Gameforge's January 2026 announcement, available in more than 16 languages (it celebrated its 19th anniversary that month). In Turkey the official forum, wiki and support (ticket) system belong to Gameforge. The game has five classes, three kingdoms and Champion levels after level 120.

The biggest news now is the Yohara Boost servers. Gameforge is running these temporary servers between 13 November 2026 at 10:00 (CET) and 24 February 2027 at 10:00. There is one server per cluster; for Turkey it is called 'Apex'. According to the announcements a second Turkish server was added and Apex stopped accepting new registrations. See which server is open for registration in the official forum announcement.

A practical note for returning players: your old character stays on whichever server it was on; the rules for moving to a new server may change. First check whether your old server is open, then whether the Yohara Boost server suits you. Reading the latest forum announcements before deciding takes a few minutes but saves hours of lost effort.

Tip

The dates and server names in this section follow the official announcement. If the announcement changes, go by it.

Before you start: account, server, cost

#

You need a free Gameforge account to play. You launch the game from the official play page or the Gameforge client. When registering, confirm your e-mail and check the security settings.

Turkish servers are listed on the wiki's 'Türkiye Sunucuları' page. Every server has 6 channels. The newest servers are SAPPHIRE[Star] and [Safir] (opened 08.08.2025); they are followed by RUBY[Lucifer] (07.12.2023) and RUBY[Charon] (19.08.2022). Marmara, Ezel, Barbaros, Dandanakan, Arkadaşlar and Bagjanamu are classic servers; Marmara, Arkadaşlar, Dandanakan and Bagjanamu carry the 'closed server' icon. For 'past servers' the wiki says servers are not closed but merged (Fırtına, Lodos, Apex, Zephyr, Anadolu, ONYX Bazalt, EMERALD Alesta). Servers' fee conditions are not officially stated.

When choosing a server look at three things: where your friends play, how crowded the server is and whether it has a special event calendar (the wiki marks this with an 'event server' icon). Trade is easy on a crowded server but competition is hard; on a small server it is the other way round.

Watch out

The system requirement figures come from PCGamingWiki (third party, old version list); The Gameforge pages publish no current list. The figures are low, but on current Windows check the installation and your graphics driver.

The first hours, step by step

#
  1. 1
    Open the account

    Create a Gameforge account, confirm the e-mail and review the security settings.

  2. 2
    Pick a server

    If you are new and want quick progress, consider the Yohara Boost server; if you want a permanent character, pick a normal server. Choose the same server as your friends.

  3. 3
    Choose kingdom and class

    Choose a kingdom (Shinsoo, Chunjo or Jinno) and a class. Class choice is explained in the table.

  4. 4
    Do the first quests

    The starter quests teach the basic systems and give rewards. Milestone quests at levels 10, 30, 60, 90 and 110 offer rewards (on the Yohara server).

  5. 5
    Horse system

    The game has a horse system; check the in-game quest log and the wiki for how to unlock it.

  6. 6
    Pick skills

    Do not spread skills at random; choose a single main path first.

In the first hours the aim is to learn the game's systems as much as to level: potion use, item upgrading, trade and skill growth.

As soon as you enter the game, open your inventory, the skill window and the quest log and look around once. Knowing what each key does is the biggest time saver in the first hours. Also be careful with strangers in the in-game chat who ask you for something.

Tip

On the Yohara Boost server, channels 1-2 are open to everyone, 3-4 to trusted players and 5-6 to Metin+ members. As a new player start on channels 1-2.

Choosing a class and kingdom

#

There are five classes: Warrior, Ninja, Sura, Shaman and Lycan. The playstyle difference between kingdoms is also small; the real difference is where your friends and server are. For a first character Warrior is generally considered the simplest, but this is a matter of preference and depends on the state of the server.

ClassPlaystyleWho it suits
WarriorMelee, sturdy, simplePlayers who want a solid start on a first character
NinjaMobile, fast damagePlayers who like speed and movement
SuraMagic weapon, self-buffsPlayers who do not mind planning skills
ShamanSupport and spellsPlayers who like playing in a group
LycanStrong melee attacksPlayers who want a different playstyle

Skills advance past the normal levels into Master, Grand Master and Perfect Master stages. So when spreading skills early it is healthier to choose a single main path.

Tip

Do not choose a class only by watching PvP videos; for a first character, comfort in farming matters more.

Kingdoms and map structure

#

Metin2 has three kingdoms: Chunjo (yellow flag), Jinno (blue flag) and Shinsoo (red flag). The official wiki's world map lists four maps for each kingdom: two normal maps and two guild zones. The kingdom choice does not change gameplay much; the real difference is where your friends are.

The only solid ordering available is the horse quest steps: horse level 2-6 quests are done on your kingdom's Map 1, 7-8 in the Valley of Seungryong or Yongbi Desert, 9-10 on Mount Sohan or Doyyumhwan (Fireland).

Tip

Keep the wiki's world map page open before travelling and check the monster level on that map's page; spending potions on a map far above your level is inefficient.

Leveling: a fast and efficient route

#

In Metin2 you level by hunting monsters, doing quests and breaking Metin stones. Experience gain depends on the gap between you and the monster; fighting very strong monsters is not always better. Farming steadily in a zone where you do not die and do not burn potions is often more efficient.

  • On the Yohara Boost server monsters have 40 percent less health and damage and the item drop rate is doubled.
  • At level 40 and above you can get a 50 to 200 percent EXP boost for 8 hours with Uriel.
  • Tiger Power raises PvE damage by 40 percent.

The goal of the boost server is to reach Champion Level 1. When the period ends, up to two characters can be transferred to normal servers; registration runs 17 February-3 March 2027 and transfers happen at the 24 February and 3 March maintenance. Transfers to DIAMOND and Marmara are not possible.

While farming, set yourself a simple loop: go to the zone, kill monsters, return when the inventory fills, handle the items, restock potions. Moving to a zone that fits your level instead of hitting the same place for long saves both potions and time. If you die too often in a zone, it is still too early for you.

Watch out

Re-read the transfer rules in the official forum as the announced dates approach. Dates and conditions may change.

Horse: first mount and upgrading

#

The horse is both a speed and a combat tool. According to the wiki there are four types: beginner (Beginner), war (War), military (Military) and mighty war (Mighty War) horse. Each type has its own seal, food and reviving herb: for the beginner horse the seal is the Horse Picture and the food is hay, for the war horse the Armed Horse Book and carrot, for the military horse the Military Horse Book and red ginseng, for the mighty war horse the Golden Horse Book and Premium red ginseng.

Beginner horse2-10Quest steps: 2-6 on Map 1, 7-8 in the Valley of Seungryong or Yongbi Desert, 9-10 on Mount Sohan or Doyyumhwan; each step needs 1 to 5 checkpoints
War horse12-20Monster hunting: 12 needs Bo x5, 13 Chuong x5, 14 Elite Orc General x10, 15 Black Orc x10, 16 Dark Tormentor x10, 17 Claw Poison Spider x10, 18 Desert Outlaw x20, 19 Ice Golem x10, 20 Fighting Tiger x20
Military horse21-40EXP, Yang and fodder: level 21 needs 12,500 EXP, 500,000 Yang, 50 fodder; Yang rises by 500,000 per step, from level 30 it is 5,000,000 Yang and 50 fodder; 40 is the maximum
Mighty war horseAbove the military horseUses the Golden Horse Book and Premium red ginseng; details are not officially stated

Summoning and stats: the Call Horse success chance is 10% at level 0, 15% at 1, 20% at 2 and 30% at 3; after level 4 it rises by 10 points per level and reaches 100% at level 10. The horse's stats rise with its level: at level 1 VIT 18, INT 9, STR 26, DEX 35; at level 10 23, 12, 35, 46; at level 20 32, 16, 48, 64.

Tip

Military horse steps are expensive: after level 30 each step needs 5,000,000 Yang and 50 fodder. Before you start upgrading the horse, split your Yang budget between gear upgrades and the horse.

Yang and economy: in-game routes only

#

The in-game currency is Yang. Yang is spent on potions, item upgrading, horse growth and trade. Before selling drops to an NPC, check whether they have value among players.

  • Spend on potions and upgrades in balance; do not put all your Yang into one upgrade.
  • Upgrade the weapon first, or the armor if you spend a lot on potions.
  • Do not sell unknown materials quickly.

There is an in-game shop and it is optional. Before buying shop items, read what they give in the game.

Be patient in trade. Before selling an item, look at the market price from several players; a hasty sale often leads to a loss. Learn from the official announcement whether items from the boost server carry over to a normal server; if they do not, plan to use what you gathered there before the period ends.

Watch out

Selling or buying Yang, accounts or items for real money (RMT) breaks the rules and gets the account closed. Use only the in-game trade window.

Item upgrading: blacksmiths and options

#

Item upgrading is done at blacksmiths and each blacksmith has different rules. According to the wiki's 'Upgrade Items' page the options are:

BlacksmithWhat it upgradesFeature
BlacksmithAny item regardless of level; most epic upgradesGives the base success rate; an epic upgrade is always 100%
Seon-PyeongAny item and all epic upgradesNo level limit
Guild blacksmithNon-epic itemsCosts 10% more Yang; the guild picks only one type (armour, weapon or jewellery)
Serpent Temple smithGloves onlyNo upgrade items needed; on failure the glove is not destroyed, its grade drops by 1
Demon Tower blacksmithNon-epic items up to level 90No upgrade items needed; 10% extra success chance over the Blacksmith and Seon-Pyeong

A numeric list of upgrade items and success rates is not officially stated. The fact that the page specifically says the glove is not destroyed on failure at the Serpent Temple suggests that other upgrades can lose the item on failure (this is an inference).

Watch out

Before upgrading a valuable item, make your first attempts on worthless items to get a feel for the rate; count the loss risk in your Yang budget and use only the in-game blacksmiths.

Dragon stone alchemy and biologist quests

#

Dragon Stone Alchemy (DSA) is the system for improving dragon stones. According to the wiki it first arrived in version 13.3, the Mythic class was added in 19.0 and the Amethyst stone in 23.2. Stones are upgraded by going to the Alchemist NPC and choosing 'Dragon Stone Refinement'.

UpgradeStones neededOn failure
Class: Rough, Cut, Rare, Antique, Legendary, Mythic2 stones of the same class for each stepOne stone of the same class remains
Clarity2 stones of the same clarity for each stepOne stone of the same clarity remains

The page also has headings on stone activation, time extension and removing stones; learn their details from the Alchemist in game or from the wiki page. For biologist quests the wiki main page gives a separate 'Biologist Quests' link; level and reward figures change with patches, so check that page for current values.

Tip

Every upgrade attempt costs two stones and on failure only one comes back; so try after collecting a pair of the same class or clarity.

After 120: Champion levels and Yohara

#

After level 120 the Champion levels begin, and Yohara content opens at Champion Level 1 and above. The goal of the boost server is to reach this point. Dungeons, bosses, pets and equipment upgrading come to the front at this stage.

StageWhat you doNote
1-120Leveling, quests, breaking Metin stonesThe Yohara server speeds up this stage
Champion 1+Yohara content, bosses and dungeonsThe goal of the boost server
BeyondEquipment, pets, tradeDepends on the server economy
  • Set Bonus and Set Effect (set bonuses)
  • Energy System and Pet System
  • Costume System, Aura Outfit System and Shoulder Sash System
  • Relic System, Binding System and Belt System
  • Gaya System and Monster Card System
  • Private Shop and Auto-Hunt

Each of the systems above has its own page on the official wiki; do not try to learn them all at once. Tie the system you need to a goal (e.g. upgrades and set bonuses for gear, the horse for movement).

When you reach the Champion levels, make your goal clear: equipment, dungeons or trade? Trying to run all three at once tires most players. It is healthier to pick one goal and finish it, then move to the next. Because the Yohara server is time-limited, set your goal early.

Tip

Follow events and reward campaigns in the official forum announcements; dates may change.

Common mistakes and safety

#
  • Fighting monsters above your strength and running out of potions.
  • Selling every item you see to an NPC.
  • Using the same password on other sites.

Watch out

Stay away from cheats, bots, macros, exploits, private servers, account and Yang selling (RMT) and pirated clients. They lead to account closure and theft of your data. Remember that the Metin+ channels exist to reduce bots.

For account safety, put a strong password on your e-mail account too and enable two-step verification if you can; when a Gameforge account is taken over, the e-mail is usually the weak link. For a suspicious login or a loss, contact the official support center; do not believe 'we will recover your account' promises from anywhere else.

Gameforge will not ask for your password or security code. Log in only on official addresses and do not click suspicious links.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is Metin2 free?

Yes, it is free to play. The in-game shop is optional; shop prices are not officially stated.

What is the Yohara Boost server?

A temporary server running from 13 November 2026 to 24 February 2027 that speeds up progress.

Which class should I start with?

Warrior is usually the simplest, but choose the playstyle you like.

Can I move my character to a normal server later?

Yes, up to two characters. See the official announcement for conditions.

What are the channels for?

1-2 are open to everyone, 3-4 to trusted players and 5-6 to Metin+ members.

Is the game available in Turkish?

The Turkish version's forum, wiki and support system are in Turkish; PCGamingWiki's language list also has Turkish. Confirm the in-game interface language on the official page.

What are the system requirements?

According to PCGamingWiki the minimum is Windows 7, Pentium 3 1.0 GHz, 512 MB RAM, 32 MB video memory and 2 GB disk; recommended Windows 8, Pentium 4 1.8 GHz, 1 GB RAM, 64 MB video memory and 3 GB disk. Gameforge publishes no current list.

How do I get a horse?

The beginner horse is upgraded with quest steps (levels 2-10), the war horse by monster hunting (12-20), the military horse with EXP, Yang and fodder (21-40).
