Where Path of Exile 2 stands now

#

Path of Exile 2 is a dark action role-playing game made by Grinding Gear Games. Surviving takes careful play: instead of just cutting enemies down, you plan around escapes, positioning and skill links. It is a separate game from the first Path of Exile, with its own classes and story. The pace of the game is heavier than the original Path of Exile; every fight is a chain of decisions, which can be deep for those who like it and tiring for those who do not.

The game is still in Early Access (since 6 December 2024). The current patch is the 0.5.5 Forbidden Rites event league (live since 4 September 2026). The 1.0 full release starts on 11 December 2026 (PST) on PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S and will be free for everyone (Inven Global, the official full-release page). 1.0 brings Acts 5 and 6 (24 new areas, 18 unique bosses, more than 20 named rare encounters), the Shadow and Duelist classes, the Conquerors of Wraeclast challenge league and a big endgame overhaul. How Early Access characters carry over to 1.0 is not clear in the sources used here; treat it as not officially stated and look at the official announcement closer to 1.0.

Tip

If you plan to wait until 11 December, the basic logic in this guide (resistances, linked skills, endgame) will still hold in 1.0. Which class is strongest changes from league to league; do not chase that.

Before you start: access, platform and requirements

#

To play until 11 December 2026 you need an Early Access key or a Supporter Pack; the game becomes free when 1.0 launches. On PC it runs through Steam, Epic or the game's own standalone launcher; console support for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S arrives with 1.0. The game is marked Verified on Steam Deck.

The first hours: step by step

#

At first glance the game can feel heavy to a beginner. The order below is a plain path for the first day. In the first hours your goal is not to finish fast but to get to know the system: checking the dodge roll, the gem sockets and the resistance display once in each area is enough.

  1. 1
    Pick a class

    Look at the class section and choose what suits your taste. You can also try the first few minutes of the game for a short preview; deleting a character is free.

  2. 2
    Play the campaign

    Follow the story and move region by region. The campaign feeds you experience and items while preparing you for the endgame.

  3. 3
    Socket your skill gems

    Skill gems attach to your weapon and gear. You strengthen each main skill by adding support gems to it.

  4. 4
    Use the dodge move

    The game has a dodge roll; dodging deadly attacks matters more than just hitting.

  5. 5
    Track your resistances

    Fire, cold, lightning and chaos resistances are your most important defense; check resistances whenever you change gear.

  6. 6
    Compare new gear

    Before wearing a piece, compare it to your current piece for resistances and main stats.

  7. 7
    Stay ready

    Watch your health, Spirit and mana; when they run out you cannot use skills.

Tip

If one area keeps killing you, do not keep pushing until it breaks. Something is missing from your gear, resistances or dodging; stop and look.

Class choice: 8 classes (Duelist and Shadow join with 1.0)

#

There are currently eight classes. Each class has two or three ascendancy branches; the first goal is to like a class, and to choose the ascendancy later. An ascendancy is the special power path a character picks at a certain point, unlocked by finishing the trials. With 1.0 the Shadow (daggers, traps and stealth) and Duelist classes will be added too; the ascendancies of these two classes have not been officially announced yet, so they are not in the table. Choosing a class does not lock you in; opening a new character with a different class is free.

Tip

A practical principle for a new player: on your first character pick the weapon type you feel comfortable with rather than a complicated class. Ranged ones like bows or spears are often easier to learn.

Progression path: from campaign to endgame

#
  • Campaign: advance act by act; each act ends with a big boss. In 1.0 Acts 5 and 6 will be added, completing the campaign.
  • Ascendancy trials: you unlock your ascendancy branch by completing trials. These sections are unforgiving; prepare your defenses.
  • Atlas and endgame: after the campaign you move to the map system (the Atlas). With waystones you enter areas, cut down tougher enemies and collect better gear.
  • Economy and trade: the in-game currency system is the basis of item trading. Trading is done only through the game's own mechanism.
  • League system: when new leagues arrive you start fresh with new characters; the first hours always feel as fresh.

Endgame details change from patch to patch (0.5.0 redesigned the endgame and overhauled Trial of Chaos). So instead of memorizing, it is more accurate to read the current patch notes. If you plan to wait for 1.0, trying the first four acts of the campaign now to see which class suits you is good preparation; the campaign will be completed with Acts 5 and 6.

Gear, skill links and resource management

#
  • Resistances: there are four resistance types (fire, cold, lightning, chaos); do not enter a hard area before getting them to a sufficient level.
  • Skill gem logic: a main skill gem plus support gems forms a link; support gems change how the skill behaves.
  • Spirit: a resource reserved for permanently active skills and minions; if you turn on too many, Spirit runs out.
  • Weapon commitment: each class favors a different weapon type (mace, staff, crossbow, bow, spear); choose your weapon to fit your skill.
  • Currency: the orbs used to strengthen gear are limited; think about what to spend them on.

Simple rule: do not spend currency on something you do not understand. Before trying an irreversible action that can lose an item, a new player should understand what it changes. The strength of endgame gear often lies not in a single piece but in a set of pieces that balance resistances, Spirit and damage together. Before selling an item, read the warnings on your account and stash.

The skill gem and support gem system

#

In Path of Exile 2 your abilities come from gems socketed into your weapons and armor. A skill gem gives you an attack or spell; a support gem links to that skill and changes its behavior, for example multiplying the projectile or adding damage. The number of supports you can link to a skill gem is limited and set by the sockets (numbers change with patches). So when choosing a skill, look not only at its damage but also at which supports it works well with; a good pairing is often more efficient than a strong skill on its own.

  1. 1
    Get a skill gem

    Skill gems drop from items and rewards through the campaign; choose a main skill that suits your class.

  2. 2
    Socket it

    Place the gem in a socket on your weapon or armor; each socket holds a skill and its linked supports.

  3. 3
    Add support gems

    Add support gems linked to the main skill; each support can also change the Spirit or mana cost.

  4. 4
    Watch the Spirit budget

    Permanently active skills and minions spend Spirit; when Spirit runs out you cannot turn on a new permanent skill.

  5. 5
    Test your skill

    Do a short trial in an area; if it does not work, swap the support gems.

Tip

Do not build many skills at once. Start with one main skill and two helper skills; the system is easy to learn but deep.

Watch out

Trying a hard boss or map with low resistance often ends in one hit. Close your resistance gap first, then add damage.

Campaign acts and endgame maps

#

The campaign currently consists of Acts 1 to 4 and each act ends with a big boss. With 1.0, Acts 5 and 6 are added to complete the story (Inven Global). After the campaign you move to the map system called the Atlas:

StageWhat happensWhat it is for
Campaign actsYou follow the story area by areaLevel, gear and skill base
Ascendancy trialsYou unlock the ascendancy branchThe class's real power
WaystonesUsed to enter areas on the AtlasHarder enemies and better gear
Bosses and eventsSpecial bosses and events on the AtlasValuable rewards and economy

After you finish the campaign, the game can be replayed at a higher difficulty step. The new difficulty on the maps tests the balance of your defense and damage; do not rush into high maps. The tiers of waystones and the details of events on the map will change in 1.0; official numbers about the Atlas and the endgame overhaul have not been announced yet. Wait for the 1.0 patch notes for current information.

Common beginner mistakes

#
  • Entering a hard area without looking at resistances.
  • Not using the dodge roll; trying to tank every attack.
  • Socketing skill gems at random; not checking that linked gems fit together.
  • Spending valuable currency without knowing what it does.
  • Dropping a class right away because it looks weak; the problem is often the build or missing gear.

Watch out

Some actions in the game cannot be undone (item corruption, permanent changes). Do not try an action you are unsure of on your main item.

Account security and a free-to-play warning after 1.0

#

Your Path of Exile account carries your items and progress. Do not share your password, account details or verification codes with anyone. When free play starts, a flood of new players will arrive, so fake gifts, fake trades and account-stealing attempts may rise. Add two-step verification to your account, do not use your password anywhere else, and refuse if someone in the game asks you for a password, a code or to log in on an outside site. Official support never asks you for your password.

  • Turn on two-step verification and use a separate password for each service.
  • Bots, auto-play software and cheat software break the game rules and lead to a permanent account ban.
  • Buying or selling items or currency for real money (RMT) is forbidden; sites selling cheap currency steal accounts.
  • During trades do not click links from strangers; fake trade sites are set up to steal your password.

Watch out

Because the game will be free, fake free-key and free-currency sites may grow in number. No legitimate source asks you for a password; refuse a suspicious offer.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is Path of Exile 2 free?

Right now an Early Access key is needed. Free-to-play starts with 1.0 on 11 December 2026.

Is the game in Turkish?

No. Turkish is not listed on Steam; the game is played in English.

Can I play on console?

It comes to PS5 and Xbox Series X|S with 1.0; the current Early Access is on PC.

Should I wait for 1.0?

You do not have to, but Early Access characters are not carried into the 1.0 economy. Read the official announcement before deciding.

When do the new classes arrive?

Duelist and Shadow come with 1.0.

Does it run on Steam Deck?

Yes, the game is marked Verified for Steam Deck.
