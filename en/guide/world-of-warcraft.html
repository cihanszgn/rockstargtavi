Where the game stands in 2026

#

World of Warcraft is Blizzard's online role-playing game, running since 2004. The version live today is Midnight: the 11th expansion launched on 2 March 2026 and raised the level cap to 90. New this time are the Haranir race, a third Demon Hunter specialization called Devourer, player housing, the Prey system, short dungeon-like Delves and the new hub city Silvermoon.

On live servers patch 12.1.5 arrived on 13-14 October 2026 as the mid-season update of Midnight Season 2, which started in August. The next patch, 12.1.7, is on the public test realm (PTR) and had no announced release date in mid-October. Follow the in-game news screen and Blizzard's news page for current dates.

Tip

Patch numbers and season dates change often. The version information here is as of 14 November 2026.

Before you start: account, cost, PC

#

You need a free Battle.net account and the Battle.net desktop app. New accounts get a free trial: up to level 20, including the dungeons at those levels. The free account has no guild, unlimited chat or Auction House.

Beyond level 20 you need a subscription or Game Time. The Battle.net shop says the subscription covers levels 1-80 in World of Warcraft, 1-60 in WoW: Forever, 1-60 in Classic and 1-90 in Mists of Pandaria Classic. The Midnight expansion is sold separately; you need it to go past 80 and to play Midnight content. Cancelling is done in account management; renewal is automatic.

Turkey prices (latest official notice as of November 2026): Blizzard applied new prices for Turkey from 17 January 2025. No newer Turkey notice was found; the amount in the shop may have changed, so check the basket before paying.

System requirements (Midnight, list on the Blizzard support page): the minimum is Windows 10 64-bit (May 2019 Update or newer), a 6-core processor with up to 4.0 GHz boost (Intel Coffee Lake or AMD Zen 2 and newer), a 4 GB DirectX 12 graphics card (GeForce GTX 10 series, Radeon RDNA 1 or Intel Iris Xe2), 8 GB RAM, 128 GB free SSD space and broadband internet. The recommended setup asks for Windows 11, an 8 performance-core processor with up to 5.2 GHz (Core Ultra Series 2 or Ryzen Zen 5), an 8 GB graphics card (RTX 40 series, RDNA 3 or Arc B series) and 16 GB RAM. A separate realm list for Turkish accounts is not published in an official source; price notices are made on the EU forums, so players from Turkey follow the European region's announcements. The first hours, step by step

#
  1. I
    Create and secure the account

    Create a Battle.net account, confirm your e-mail, then turn on two-step verification in the account settings.

  2. II
    Install the game

    In the Battle.net app choose World of Warcraft and install the main game (Retail). The download is big, so prepare free disk space and a solid connection.

  3. III
    Pick a race and class

    New players are sent to a short starting zone called Exile's Reach while creating a character. It teaches the basic controls.

  4. IV
    Follow the guidance

    After the start the game points you to an expansion story. Blizzard describes new players being carried to level 80 through an accelerated The War Within story, which also summarizes the story before Midnight.

  5. V
    Try the Group Finder

    Queue for dungeons that suit your level through the Group Finder. It gives quick experience and teaches the rhythm of the game.

Do not rush on day one. If you dislike a class after an hour or two, creating another character is allowed.

Tip

When your bags fill, visit a vendor; grey items can be sold. Before selling blue and purple items, check whether your character can use them.

#

Tip

Trying different classes on the same account is allowed. Most players create a few characters and keep the one they enjoy most.

Leveling: a fast and efficient route

#

Between levels 10 and 70, Chromie Time lets you choose which expansion's zones you level in. New players are pointed to Dragonflight by default. Each expansion tells its own story and you do not have to finish them all.

  • Midnight leveling dungeons: Windrunner Spire, Murder Row, Den of Nalorakk, Maisara Caverns and The Blinding Vale.
  • Skyriding (fast gliding flight) is available from the start and helps a lot with travel and gathering.
  • Finishing the Midnight main story unlocks Adventure Mode for your other characters.

To progress efficiently, do the quests of a zone together, queue for dungeons, and do not leave an expansion story half done. On the free trial you stop at level 20; that is the moment to decide on a subscription or Game Time.

Dungeons have three roles: tank, healer and damage dealer. Tank and healer queues are usually shorter, so if you can play one of them, finding a dungeon gets faster. Even as a beginner you will soon see why these roles are in demand. Renew your gear regularly too: dungeon and quest rewards carry you while leveling, and there is no point keeping old items in your bags.

Tip

If you are returning, your talent tree and class mechanics may have changed. Review your talents when you load in.

Professions: what they do and which to pick

#

Each character picks two primary professions; you can also learn secondary ones such as cooking, fishing and archaeology freely. Professions fall into two groups: gathering professions give raw materials and crafting professions turn them into items. Since you gather materials while levelling anyway, pairing one gathering and one crafting profession is the most efficient start.

GroupProfessionsWhat it gives
GatheringHerbalism, Mining, SkinningRaw materials; can be sold on the Auction House
CraftingAlchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Inscription, Jewelcrafting, Leatherworking, TailoringPotions, armor, weapons, enchants, engineering gadgets
SecondaryCooking, Fishing, ArchaeologyFood bonuses and cosmetic rewards
  • Herbalism with Alchemy, and Mining with Blacksmithing or Jewelcrafting, are natural pairs.
  • Skinning and Leatherworking share the same material; you can sell it or craft for yourself.
  • Enchanting disenchants items into enchanting materials; putting enchants on gear depends on it.

You do not have to choose a profession on day one; in the free trial you can try the first ranks. You can change profession later but the progress is reset, so make a small trial before deciding. The real economic value changes with each expansion, so follow current profession guides in the game and on the Blizzard news page.

Tip

If you chose a gathering profession, make it a habit to pick plants and veins on the way during quests; you will not need separate farming later.

Content tiers: Delves, dungeons, raids, Mythic+

#

After level 90 the game progresses by difficulty tiers. The easiest entry is Delves: underground scenarios for one player or a small group. Then come dungeons (5 players), raids (large-group content, up to 20 players) and the hardest, Mythic+.

ContentPlayersDifficulty tiersWhat for
Delves1-5 (usually solo with a companion)Tier stepsShort, quick and comfortable rewards
Dungeon5Normal, Heroic, MythicLearning team play
Mythic+5Keystone level (+2, +3...)Challenging, weekly reward
RaidLarge groupLFR, Normal, Heroic, MythicStory peak and powerful gear

LFR (Looking for Raid) is the easiest raid difficulty, entered from a queue. To get used to team play, start in dungeons on Normal and progress towards Heroic and Mythic. Mythic+ makes the Mythic dungeon harder with a keystone level and ties it to a timer; entering unprepared tires both you and your team.

Tip

Delves are a safe training ground for a first character: you do not wait for a group and you do not hold anyone back while learning.

Watch out

If you accidentally ruin a queued dungeon you are not punished, but if you leave again and again the group finder may restrict you for a while. Raise the difficulty at your own pace.

Weekly to-do list

#

At level 90 the game settles into a weekly rhythm. The reset day varies by region; in Europe it is usually Wednesday morning; this may change with patches. The list below gathers one week of work after reset:

  • Check the weekly main quest and world quests; pick the ones with rewards.
  • Do a few rounds of Delves; they count for the weekly vault reward.
  • Do your Mythic+ keystone or dungeon completions; they also count for the vault.
  • Try at least one boss from the raid queue (LFR or Normal).
  • Choose and collect your reward from the Great Vault.
  • Check the profession weekly quests and craft order opportunities.

The Great Vault opens three reward slots depending on how many dungeons, raids and Delves you did that week. You choose only one from each slot. If you do a few activities of all three content types, you collect the vault at its fullest. This structure rests on the weekly pattern introduced in Blizzard's Midnight briefing (Blizzard News: Midnight expansion information).

Tip

Write the list on paper or on your phone and tick it each week. It becomes a habit after a few weeks; most players finish it in only 1-2 hours.

Gold and economy: in-game routes only

#

The right way to earn gold is the game's own systems. The Auction House enables trade between players and depends on a subscription. Selling gathered materials (herbs, ore, leather) there is a safe income for a new player.

  • Before handing quest rewards and dungeon drops to a vendor, check the Auction House price.
  • Pick a gathering profession early; you gather materials while leveling anyway.
  • Before selling something that looks valuable, check its price history and do not jump at the first price.

Professions fall into two groups: gathering (herbs, ore, leather) and crafting (blacksmithing, alchemy, enchanting and the like). A crafting profession only works with gathered materials, so pairing the two is smart. Do not expect income on day one; building up gold takes a few weeks. Prices differ from realm to realm, so never treat any price as permanent.

Watch out

Buying or selling gold or characters on outside sites gets your account closed. Blizzard forbids it, and many of those sites are tied to account theft as well.

After the level cap: what is there to do?

#

At the level cap the game opens into layered content. Delves are short dungeons for one player or a small group, a good bridge before raiding. Mythic+ (timed dungeons) and raids give the best gear. Gain experience on Normal and Heroic difficulty first.

Player housing came with Midnight: you get a home and decorate it. The Prey system is a Midnight-specific mechanic. Its rules can change with patches, so read the details in the in-game guide.

ContentWhat forWhen
DelvesSafe progress alone or in a small groupAs you near the level cap
Mythic+Timed dungeons, high rewardsAfter Normal and Heroic
RaidLarge-group content, boss fightsWhen your item level is enough
PvPPlayer-versus-player combatOptional

Joining a guild is the biggest accelerator at this stage: finding dungeon and raid partners, asking questions and trading gear all get easier. A guild needs a subscription or Game Time. Look for one that fits your schedule and runs regular events; you do not have to stay in the first guild you try.

Tip

Track your item level; the game tells you which dungeon is suitable for you.

UI addons and the Classic versus Retail difference

#

Addons are small programs that change the game's interface. Blizzard allows only addons that change the interface and display information; addons that play for you, press keys automatically or act as bots are banned and lead to account closure. Download only from the addon's official page; a file from an unknown site can steal your account.

  • Boss warning addons (for example BigWigs or Deadly Boss Mods) show raid mechanics on time.
  • Bag, map and damage meter addons are optional for beginners; do not load yourself with addons before learning the game.
  • Update addons after a big patch; an old version can cause errors in the game.

Retail, Classic and Forever: Retail is the Midnight version described today. Classic offers older states of the game: Classic Era, Classic Progression, Anniversary Edition and Season of Discovery realms. WoW: Forever is a separate version that retells the original Azeroth with new zones, redesigned classes, over 1000 new quests and new dungeons and raids; it offers a new race called Skyborne. The same subscription gives access to all three, but the Midnight expansion is for Retail only.

Tip

If you do not know which to pick, start with the free trial on Retail; it is easy to access and has an active population.

Common mistakes and safety

#
  • Leaving the Battle.net account without two-step verification.
  • Rushing levels and entering dungeons before learning your class; do not stall the group, and say so if you are unsure.
  • Reusing the same password on several sites.

Watch out

Stay away from cheats, bots, macros, exploits, private servers, account and gold selling (RMT) and pirated software. They can lead to a permanent ban and theft of your personal data.

Blizzard will not ask for your password or verification code by e-mail. Check the address bar before clicking a suspicious link, and ignore players offering 'free gold'.

Frequently asked questions

#
Is the game free?

There is a free trial up to level 20. After that you need a subscription or Game Time; for levels above 80 you must also buy Midnight.

Can I play without buying Midnight?

Yes, up to level 80 with a subscription. The Midnight story and level 90 require the expansion.

Which class should I start with?

A plain damage class. If you do not like it, creating another character is allowed.

Will it run on an old PC?

Midnight asks for at least Windows 10 and does not support some older hardware; check the official system requirements.

I am coming back after a long time. Where do I start?

Activate a subscription or Game Time, buy Midnight, pick your character, update your talents and read the news on the main screen.

Can I play on mobile or console?

The information here is for Windows; for other platforms check Blizzard's pages.
