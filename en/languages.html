GTA 6 languages: full list of subtitles and voice options

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Quick answer

English voices, menus and subtitles in 13 languages.

According to store listings, voice acting is in English only. Menus and subtitles are available in English plus 12 other languages. Rockstar has not yet published its own official language list.

Which languages are available?

According to the Xbox store page, voice acting is in English only. Menus and subtitles are available in 12 languages besides English: French, German, Italian, Spanish (Spain and Mexico), Portuguese, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Turkish is not on the list.

This follows the pattern of Rockstar's previous games: GTA V also launched with English-only voice acting and text in 13 languages. No Rockstar game has received official Turkish language support to date.

Source: gtasix.org (Xbox store data, September 29, 2026) · BBC Türkçe

The Turkish subtitle campaign

Turkish players have gathered more than 23,000 signatures for Turkish subtitles on openPetition. Read more on the Turkish language page.

Frequently asked questions

Is English voice acting available? Are there other dubs?

Yes, the voice acting is in English. There are no dubs in other languages; voice acting is English only.

How do I select English menus and subtitles?

The language is generally selected automatically from your console's system language. Set the console's system language to English for English menus and subtitles.

Has a PC version been announced?

No. A PC version has not yet been announced.
