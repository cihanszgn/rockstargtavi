Analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they read. Through cookies, Google Analytics collects anonymous statistics such as browser type, device, approximate location (city/country), referring site and time spent on a page. We do not use this data to identify you personally; we only use it to understand which content visitors find useful.

To learn how Google processes data, see Google partner sites. To prevent tracking, you can disable cookies in your browser or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
