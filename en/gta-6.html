Countdown to GTA VI

Grand Theft Auto VI launches on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Release dates, editions and launch preparations, all in one place.

1days00hours00minutes00seconds

The countdown runs to midnight on November 19, Los Angeles time (PST). Unlock times may vary by platform and region.

GTA VI at a glance

Release date

November 19, 2026

The official date announced by Rockstar. The game was previously scheduled for fall 2025 and then May 26, 2026.

Platforms

PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

No official release date has been announced for a PC version.

Editions

Standard and Ultimate

US prices per Take-Two's June 24, 2026 press release: $79.99 Standard and $99.99 Ultimate. Check the PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store for local pricing.

Preload

From November 10

Players who preorder digitally can start downloading before launch.

Setting

Leonida and Vice City

The game takes place in the fictional, Florida-inspired state of Leonida.

Characters

Lucia and Jason

The story follows two protagonists: Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval.
