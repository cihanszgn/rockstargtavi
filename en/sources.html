GTA VI news sources

Last updated: November 9, 2026.

Places to look for GTA VI information, with a short note on what each is good for. This list is a starting point, not an endorsement.

Do not read rumors and leaks as official announcements. If something only circulates on community sites or social media, it is not confirmed until it comes from Rockstar.

Official sources

Rockstar Games: GTA VI

The official game page: trailers and information confirmed by Rockstar.

Rockstar Newswire

Rockstar's official announcement feed: dates, editions and update news appear here first.

Rockstar Support

The official help center for account, purchase and technical problems.

International gaming press

IGN

Broad coverage with news, previews and reviews.

GameSpot

Game news, reviews and guides.

Eurogamer

News, features and reviews, with a European perspective.

PC Gamer

PC-focused news and hardware coverage, useful for following any PC release.

Polygon

News, features and game guides.

Kotaku

News and commentary on games and gaming culture.

VGC (Video Games Chronicle)

Industry-focused video game news.

The Verge

Technology outlet that covers major game launches for a general audience.

Community and wikis

GTA Wiki (Fandom)

Community wiki about the series: characters, locations and past games.

GTAForums

The series' long-running discussion forum. Rumors circulate here too, so check the source.

r/GTA6

The GTA VI community on Reddit: fast-moving, but full of unverified claims.
