GTA VI launch preparation guide

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Before launch day

  1. Free up storage. The PS5 version is reported to be around 212 GB. Leave some extra space for updates.
  2. Start preloading if you bought digitally. Downloads begin at midnight local time on November 10, so you can avoid waiting on launch night.
  3. Redeem your code early if you bought a boxed copy. The box contains a download code, not a disc. The download starts when you redeem the code.
  4. Update your console. A system update could delay your download on launch night.
  5. Do not miss the bonus deadline. Purchases made before November 20 include the Vintage Vice City Pack.

Frequently asked questions

When does GTA VI come out?

November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The game unlocks at midnight local time; Xbox unlock times may differ in some regions.

Is GTA VI coming to PC?

Rockstar has not announced a PC release date. Previous games came to PC after consoles, but there is no official information for GTA VI. We will update this page when an announcement is made.

Can I play on PS4 or Xbox One?

No. The game has only been announced for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Where is the game set?

In the fictional state of Leonida, inspired by Florida. Vice City is part of that state.

Who are the main characters?

Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval. The story follows both of them.

Does the boxed edition include a disc?

No, the box contains a download code. You still need to download the game over the internet.
