About

CTRL+A GAME is an independent publication in English, Turkish, French and Spanish, not affiliated with any game company.

It brings together sourced game news from every platform, deals from official stores, free games, clearly labeled rumors and a release calendar. It also keeps a detailed hub for GTA VI.

Our principles

We use automation to help research and prepare our news feed. Automatically published stories undergo official-source verification, duplicate checks and technical validation; original writing and practical value for players are required.

  • News is based only on official announcements (publisher and platform blogs) or major outlets confirming them. Every story shows its source.
  • Unverified claims appear only in the Rumors section, labeled with a confidence level, a status and at least one source. They are never presented as news.
  • Deals list official and authorized digital stores only; gray-market key sellers are not included.
  • We do not share leaked content, pirated copies, cheats or mod menus, account or key sellers, or betting links.
  • News images load from the official source's server with visible attribution; our own illustrated cover appears when an image is unavailable.
  • If you spot a mistake, let us know and we will correct it right away.

Contact

For corrections, suggestions or rights-holder requests, write to us on GitHub.
