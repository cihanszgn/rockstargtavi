GTA VI news

Last updated: November 9, 2026. Based only on official announcements and reliable news outlets; rumors are not reported as news.

  • November 2026Digital preload brought forward to November 10

    Originally announced in June for November 12, preload was moved to midnight local time on November 10 in Rockstar's early November announcement. The PS5 version is reported to be around 212 GB.

    Source: PCWorld, November 5, 2026 · IGN

  • June 25, 2026Preorders open, with two editions announced

    Standard Edition ($79.99) is available digitally or as a code in a box, while Ultimate Edition ($99.99) is digital only. Purchases made before November 20 include the "Vintage Vice City Pack" bonus.

    Source: dlcompare

  • November 2025Release date moved to November 19, 2026

    Rockstar moved the release to November 19, 2026, saying it needed more time to polish the game.

    Source: Rockstar Newswire

  • May 2025Second trailer released

    The second trailer offered a closer look at Lucia, Jason and the state of Leonida.

    Source: rockstargames.com/VI

  • May 2025Launch delayed to May 26, 2026

    The release, previously planned for fall 2025, was moved to May 26, 2026.

    Source: Rockstar Newswire

    The first trailer officially introduced the game and confirmed the return to Vice City.
