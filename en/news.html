GTA VI news

Last updated: November 14, 2026. Based only on official announcements and reliable news outlets; rumors are not reported as news.

  • November 13, 2026Xbox Wire: buy by November 19 for the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+

    According to Xbox Wire's launch-week list, buying the game before 23:59:59 on November 19, 2026 includes the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+. Pre-install is open on Xbox Series X|S, and Xbox Cloud Gaming streaming starts at 1 AM ET on November 19.

    Source Xbox Wire, November 13, 2026

  • November 12, 2026YouTube hosts a 24-hour launch livestream

    According to the YouTube Blog, youtube.com/youtube will run a 24-hour stream on launch day with live gameplay, creator podcasts and original segments. A pre-show starts at 11 PM ET on November 18, the main stream begins at 12 AM ET on November 19 and it wraps with live coverage from Miami.

    Source YouTube Blog, November 12, 2026

  • November 10, 2026GTA VI comes to Xbox Cloud Gaming at launch, including Xbox One

    According to Xbox Wire, the game arrives on Xbox Cloud Gaming on November 19, so Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S owners can play it from the cloud. Streaming begins at 1 AM ET on November 19. The game must be purchased separately, and cloud availability varies by market.

    Source Xbox Wire, November 10, 2026

  • November 10, 2026Most pre-ordered game in PlayStation Store history

    According to the PlayStation Blog, GTA VI is now the most pre-ordered game in the history of PlayStation Store. Because of this demand, pre-loading opened nine days early. On PS5 the game unlocks at local midnight in each country on November 19.

    Source PlayStation Blog, November 10, 2026

  • November 10, 2026Rockstar: GTA VI targets 30 FPS on all consoles

    According to a statement on Rockstar's official website, the game targets 30 frames per second on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar explains this as pushing both platforms to their limits while preserving the density and visual quality of Leonida. There is no 60 FPS or 40 FPS performance mode; the target is 30 FPS on PS5 Pro as well.

    Source: Push Square, November 10, 2026 (reporting Rockstar's statement) · Game Rant

  • November 10, 2026DualSense, 3D audio and PS5 Pro enhancements announced for PS5

    According to the PlayStation Blog, GTA VI uses the DualSense controller's speaker, haptic feedback and adaptive triggers throughout the game on PS5; these can be changed in the accessibility settings. Game audio is positioned with Tempest 3D Audio, and load times are shorter thanks to the PS5's fast SSD. On PS5 Pro there is a sharper image with PSSR and ray-traced shadows.

    Source: PlayStation Blog, November 10, 2026 · Game Rant

  • November 9, 2026Take-Two: pre-orders at unprecedented levels

    In Take-Two's fiscal second-quarter 2027 results, CEO Strauss Zelnick said GTA VI pre-orders are "continuing to reach unprecedented levels". The company did not give an exact pre-order number; the release date remains November 19, 2026 (PS5, Xbox Series X|S).

    Source Take-Two Interactive, November 9, 2026

  • November 2026Digital preload brought forward to November 10

    Originally announced in June for November 12, preload was moved to midnight local time on November 10 in Rockstar's early November announcement. The PS5 version is reported to be around 212 GB.

    Source: PCWorld, November 5, 2026 · IGN

  • June 25, 2026Preorders open, with two editions announced

    Standard Edition ($79.99) is available digitally or as a code in a box, while Ultimate Edition ($99.99) is digital only. Purchases made before November 20 include the "Vintage Vice City Pack" bonus.

    Source: dlcompare

  • November 2025Release date moved to November 19, 2026

    Rockstar moved the release to November 19, 2026, saying it needed more time to polish the game.

    Source: Rockstar Newswire

  • May 2025Second trailer released

    The second trailer offered a closer look at Lucia, Jason and the state of Leonida.

    Source: rockstargames.com/VI

  • May 2025Launch delayed to May 26, 2026

    The release, previously planned for fall 2025, was moved to May 26, 2026.

    Source: Rockstar Newswire

  • December 2023First trailer released

    The first trailer officially introduced the game and confirmed the return to Vice City.

    Source: rockstargames.com/VI
